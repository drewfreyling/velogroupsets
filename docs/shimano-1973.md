**Policy follow-up:** the owner approved components without matching technical-document links on 21 November 2026. The [early Shimano import](shimano-early-catalogue-import.md) now adds 34 components from the reviewed 1973/1978 road spreads. The missing-document blocker described in this historical review no longer applies; source identity, membership and weight-configuration requirements still do.


Reviewed on 21 November 2026 from the [owner-supplied original Shimano catalogue](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12pM10q10Sb3kbiDgQqn5LryPQ9ZRhqu2/view). The cover says **’73 Shimano Bicycle Parts**. The scan contains **28 PDF pages**, including three memo pages at the end. References below use PDF pages; the Dura-Ace spread on PDF pages 4–5 is numbered 2–3 in print.

The original Dura-Ace groupset now cites this spread. No new component records or canonical weights are imported: matching manufacturer-hosted technical documents were not found for these early model codes. The inventory remains 262 groupsets and 1,604 components. This supporting-source review does not create an exception to the primary-document host rule.

The catalogue establishes membership of these early products in its Dura-Ace spread. It does not establish that G-210 is FC-7110, G-520 is BB-7200, or H-731/H-831 are later HB/FH models. No such aliases or launch-date changes are introduced. The [Dura-Ace range notes](dura-ace.md#1973-original-range-catalogue) retain the existing 1973 start and stable groupset identity.

The D-501 wording is more specific than the general table’s bare “Capacity” label, but it is not evidence for all four modern derailleur-limit fields. No largest/smallest sprocket or front-difference limit is inferred from that single number.

## Other derailleurs, controls and hubs

PDF pages 6–8 repeat the earlier Crane, Titlist, Tourney, Eagle, Lark and Thunderbird weight leads. See the [1972 rear table](shimano-1972.md#rear-derailleur-weight-leads) and [front configurations](shimano-1972.md#front-derailleur-leads) for the exact models and masses. The new D-501 figure on page 4 does not assign a weight to the separately coded D511, D601, D611, D701 or D711 alternatives.

The L-series controls and general H-series hubs still need exact model/configuration documents. No single control is converted into an invented pair, no H code is expanded into a later HB/FH identity, and no quick-release mass is subtracted from a hub figure. Internal-gear assembly figures do not become bare hub or brake-caliper weights.

## Rim brakes and levers

PDF page 21 / printed page 19 distinguishes:

- **B-110 CC-65**, reach 47–65 mm, and **B-120 CC-75**, reach 57–75 mm, centre-pull calipers.
- **B-220 CS-79**, reach 61–79 mm, and **B-230 CS-72**, reach 54–72 mm, side-pull calipers.
- **M-110/M-120** hooded levers for 22.2/23.8 mm clamps, and **M-210/M-220** extension levers for the same respective clamp sizes.
- **M-410** popular and **M-310** tourist levers, both with 22.2 mm clamps.

No qualifying pair/position weights are supplied. These early B/M codes are not silently converted into later BR/BL models. The B-210 on the Dura-Ace spread remains distinct from the B-220/B-230 on this general brake page.

## Mechanical and oil disc brakes

PDF page 22 / printed page 20 shows **B-700** under “Disc Brake” and **B900** under “Oil Disc Brake”. These are distinct systems. The B-700 description specifies a **7 inch / 178 mm disc** and an exclusive **36-hole rear hub**. That description is in the B-700 column; it is not a sourced rotor diameter for the B900.

No separate rotor model, caliper mass or complete hydraulic reference configuration is supplied. A photograph does not establish hose length, fluid inclusion, mounting hardware or the other fields required by the [disc-brake model](disc-brakes.md). No system weight is invented or reassigned to a caliper.

The same page lists **C100**, a coaster-brake hub assembly, at **860 g** with a **163 mm axle**, several drilling options and several sprocket choices. The actual weighed configuration and included accessories are not selected. This is not a standalone brake mass.

## Primary-document checks and outcome

Shimano’s public document search was checked for the new spread/brake codes, including **G210, G220, G520, H731, H831, L284, M140, B210, B220, B230, B900, M310, M410 and F100**, alongside D501/E304 and the earlier edition’s codes. Exact model-tag matches were not found in the reviewed results. Targeted manufacturer-site searches for G-520, Crane D-501, B-900 and the 1973 catalogue also found no matching technical document.

Substring matches are insufficient: D501 can return SH-SD501 shoe instructions, and generic Shimano history pages establish family history rather than technical specifications for these exact models. The [manufacturer Dura-Ace history](https://www.shimano.com/en/100th/history/products/14.html) remains a range-history source.

The scan is indexed, its original-range spread is cited in canonical groupset data, and its component leads are preserved here. Matching manufacturer-hosted primary documents remain the prerequisite for importing these component identities. Pair basis, crank configuration and skewer exclusions would still require evidence even if the document-host gap were resolved.
