# Shimano MTB and microSHIFT catalogue additions

Source review on 21 November 2026. The first batch adds seven Shimano component records, including six manufacturer-listed weights, and fills four existing microSHIFT weight gaps. In that first batch, existing component IDs, slugs, memberships and primary technical documents remain unchanged. The follow-up below documents further additions, an existing shared headset's LX membership and a conflicting weight withheld.

## Shimano 1991 dealer manual

The manufacturer-hosted [1991 dealer product manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf) identifies the following variants explicitly. PDF pagination below refers to that official scan; other scans of the publication can differ.

| Model | Weight | Total capacity | Largest sprocket | Smallest sprocket | Front difference | PDF / printed page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RD-M735-SGS | 270 g | 38T | 32T | 12T | 20T | 52 / 49 |
| RD-M735-SS | 250 g | 28T | 28T | 12T | Not specified for SS | 52 / 49 |
| RD-M650-SGS | 320 g | 38T | 32T | 12T | 20T | 59 / 56 |
| RD-M650-SS | 257 g | 28T | 28T | 12T | Not specified for SS | 59 / 56 |
| RD-M550-SGS | 320 g | 38T | 32T | 12T | 20T | 65 / 62 |
| RD-M550-GS | Not yet sourced | 36T | 30T | 12T | 20T | 65 / 62 |

All six are documented as compatible with six- and seven-speed SIS. These are actual manufacturer cage suffixes, not invented model numbers. The generic RD-M735, RD-M650 and RD-M550 records retain unknown weights and their stable identities. Only limits common to their documented cage variants are filled there: the 12T minimum sprocket in all three, and the 20T front difference for RD-M550. Variant-dependent limits remain null in those generic records. The manual assigns the XT and DX 20T front difference specifically to SGS, so the SS fields were left unknown in that pass. The later [1990 table review](shimano-mtb-1990.md) establishes a separately printed 13T limit for RD-M650-SS; the XT SS value remains unknown. No replacement limit is calculated.

The LX section also lists **SL-M453-A at 184 g explicitly for the pair, without brake levers** on PDF page 66 / printed page 63. The new record represents the complete left/right seven-speed thumb-shifter pair with 22.2 mm handlebar clamps, not an inferred doubled individual weight.

The normal-band FD-M735, FD-M650 and FD-M550 figures on the same pages do not identify which clamp diameter was weighed. Those front-derailleur weights remain unknown.

## microSHIFT 2025 catalogue

The manufacturer's [PDF catalogue index](https://www.microshift.com/pdf-catalog/) links the [official 2025 catalogue](https://www.microshift.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Microshift-2025-Catalog-20240911.pdf). Direct downloads returned a verification page during this review. The original **2025 Drivetrain** publication was read in the [Bike Catalogs archived copy](https://www.bikecatalogs.com/download/5815), with its year/cover and model tables checked visually. That copy has 43 PDF spreads; the printed page numbers identify the individual pages within each spread. It is not assumed to be byte-identical to the official download.

| Existing model / configuration | Weight added | PDF spread / printed page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RD-M6205AM-B, Ver.2 aluminum-cage 1×10-speed clutch derailleur | 302 g | 6 / 8 |
| CS-H104, ten-speed 11–48T ED Black steel cassette | 662 g | 6 / 8 |
| SL-M6185-R, single right-hand eight-speed Quick Trigger Pro, rubber pad, no indicator | 122 g | 8 / 11 |
| CS-H083, eight-speed 12–46T ED Black cassette | 576 g | 8 / 12 |

The cassette records already identify every sprocket tooth count. Lockring inclusion is assumed under the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md#cassettes). SL-M6185-R retains its genuine single right-hand 1×8 identity; the pair rule does not apply.

The **387 g RD-M5185M-B** entry describes the Ver.2 clutch model, so it is not transferred to the existing original RD-M5185M SpringLock record. Its weight remains null. The 505 g CS-H083 entries describe 12–42T and 11–42T configurations, not the catalogue's existing 12–46T record. Manufacturer-hosted primary technical documents remain unchanged; supporting archive citations do not extend the primary-document archive exception.

## Follow-up: further weights and original catalogue scans

A subsequent pass adds **seven further manufacturer-listed weights**: two previously unknown microSHIFT triple-shifter pairs and five new Shimano component records. It also documents LX membership for the existing HP-R500 headset and withholds one conflicting cassette weight. This brings the catalogue to 1,400 component records; these figures describe the follow-up, separately from the first batch above.

| Model / configuration | Weight | Original source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SL-M850-3, complete 3×10-speed Xpress Plus pair, without gear indicators | 225 g | microSHIFT 2025, PDF spread 11 / printed page 18 |
| SL-M760-3, complete 3×10-speed Xpress Plus pair, with gear indicators | 286 g | microSHIFT 2025, PDF spread 11 / printed page 18 |
| PD-M737, left/right SPD pedal pair, Cro-Mo axles | 450 g | Shimano 1991, PDF page 56 / printed page 53 |
| PD-M735, left/right super-low-profile alloy pedal pair | 295 g | Shimano 1991, PDF page 56 / printed page 53 |
| PD-M730, left/right low-profile alloy pedal pair | 412 g | Shimano 1991, PDF page 56 / printed page 53 |
| PD-M550, left/right resin-body pedal pair with steel plates | 326 g | Shimano 1991, PDF page 69 / printed page 66 |
| CS-HG70, seven-speed E-group 12–28T, including spacers | 325 g | Shimano 1991, PDF pages 62 and 68 / printed pages 59 and 65 |

The microSHIFT table explicitly lists each triple model's weight, resolving the older catalogue's ambiguity without transferring double-model figures or doubling a right-hand control. The Shimano pedal figures are explicitly pair weights; cleat, toe-clip and reflector inclusions are disclosed as unspecified where the source does not establish them. PD-M737, PD-M735 and PD-M730 are listed in the 1991 XT section and are attached to the M735-era group; the model number alone is not used to infer membership of another generation. The E-group cassette is shared by the documented DX M650 and LX M550 ranges, with 12/14/16/18/21/24/28T sprockets. It is distinct from the existing I-group cassette.

**CS-HG90 I-group conflict:** the original 1990 600 Ultegra reference lists 286 g for 13–23T sprockets and spacers, but the 1991 XT table on PDF page 55 / printed page 52 gives 325 g for the same I-group configuration. No distinguishing assembly is established. Its stable record and original primary technical document are preserved, both sources are retained, and `weight_g` is now null rather than selecting or averaging the conflicting figures. The existing CS-HG70 I-group weight likewise remains unknown.

**HP-R500 membership:** the 1991 LX page 69 lists the same model at 121 g with 22 mm upper and 11.5 mm lower cone dimensions. LX membership is added to the shared record without duplicating its identity or changing its weight. Its descriptive name becomes “Shimano threaded headset”, since the manufacturer also lists it for off-road use. The weighed thread variant remains unspecified.
