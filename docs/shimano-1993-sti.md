# Shimano 1993 STI Exage ES / LT inventory and weights

Reviewed on **23 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 1993 Shimano STI manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1993_Shimano_STI.pdf), cross-checked against the original [1993 dealer product manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1993_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manuall.pdf). The STI PDF has **79 pages**. Selected STI citations identify PDF and printed page numbers separately; dealer PDF and printed numbers match.

This pass adds **two MTB groupsets, 22 components and seven sourced weights**, and enriches **seven existing component identities**. Exage ES M520 has **15 components** and Exage LT M320 has **18**, including shared parts. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, **274 groupsets and 2,041 components**. Existing IDs, model numbers, primary technical documents and known masses are preserved.

## Ranges and identities

The [STI front-drive sheet, PDF 10 / printed 9](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1993_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=10), and [brake sheet, PDF 15 / printed 14](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1993_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=15), explicitly name **Exage ES** and **Exage LT**. The original dealer tables independently identify [ES on page 31](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1993_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manuall.pdf#page=31) and [LT on page 33](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1993_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manuall.pdf#page=33).

Both ranges use **1993 as their first reviewed dated availability reference**, not a claimed introduction year. Their generation notes state that limitation. These are MTB ranges, separate from the existing road Exage Sport, Motion and EX groupsets.

The STI rear-drive sheet's M320 column says **“Exage LX”**, while the front/brake sheets and the complete dealer spread say **“Exage LT”**. This review uses the independently corroborated LT name and cites the inconsistent rear-drive heading explicitly; it does not create an additional LX range from that one column.

| Range | Added identities |

| Exage ES M520 | RD-M520-GS / SGS, FD-M520 / FD-M521, FC-M520, ST-M520, BR-M520 / BR-M521, CS-HG70-7 E 12–28T, shared HP-R501 |
| Exage LT M320 | RD-M320-GS / SGS, FD-M320 / FD-M321, FC-M320, ST-M320, BR-M320 / BR-M321, SL-M201, BL-CT10, CS-HG50 M 13–28T, HB-RM50, shared HP-R501 |

The unsuffixed **HB-RM50** in the LT table remains separate from the explicitly printed **HB-RM50-F** in ES. No source establishes a suffix alias in this pass, and the existing contributor's 139 g / 36-hole HB-RM50-F measurement is neither moved nor copied.

| Exact reference | Weight | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RD-M520-SGS | 301 g | Dealer page 31 explicitly selects SGS; no GS mass is inferred |

| ST-M520 | 395 g per pair | Dealer page 31 identifies a complete 3×7 combined-control set; matching front/rear STI sheets name the same exact model |
| SL-M201 | 154 g per pair | Dealer page 33 explicitly prints “pair” for this seven-speed control |

The scanned ST-M320 and CS-HG50 M masses remain insufficiently clear at close inspection; those two records retain unknown weights. Their model/configuration facts are legible, and no neighbouring mass is substituted.

No single-side control figure is doubled. The ST-M520/ST-M320 references use the documented complete front/rear control sets under the contribution rule; SL-M201 has an explicit pair label. SL-M201's front indexing is not separately specified, so its record does not invent a front-speed field.

Both cranks select **170 mm** while retaining the available 165/175 mm lengths. The service sheet supplies **110/74 mm bolt circles**; these are not copied from the later 95/58 mm STX models. All crank weights exclude bottom brackets. Cassette lockring inclusion follows the contribution convention. The E and M configurations remain separate from existing CS-HG50 I 13–23T, RSX 11–24T and other HG70 references.

## Directly sourced specifications

For both M520 and M320, the [rear-drive sheet, PDF 13 / printed 12](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1993_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=13), and matching dealer tables establish:

| Cage | Total capacity | Largest sprocket | Smallest sprocket | Maximum front difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GS | 36T | 30T | 12T | 20T |
| SGS | 38T | 32T | 12T | 20T |

All four limits are transcribed directly; the dealer tables supply the front difference missing from the service summary. The GS and SGS assemblies keep distinct identities. Only the SGS records receive the explicitly assigned masses.

FD-M520 / FD-M320 are normal bottom-route models; FD-M521 / FD-M321 are the separately listed top-route models. The original tables give **24T BioPace / 26T round-chainring capacity** as separate conditional values. All four retain their actual available **28.0–28.6 / 31.8 / 34.9 mm bands**. A bottom-route model's unqualified mass does not identify the weighed band and is not transferred to a top-route model, so these four weights stay unknown.

BR-M520 and BR-M320 have **M arms**; BR-M521 and BR-M321 have **L arms**, all with dynamic-type unit links. They are complete front/rear cantilever-pair references with unknown weights pending explicit pad/bolt inclusion. BL-CT10 is a standalone brake-lever pair, distinct from combined ST-M320 controls. Its weight is also unknown.

## Shared parts and preserved references

Existing **FH-HG50**, **HB-RM50-F**, **HP-R500**, **PD-M350**, **BB-UN51**, **BB-LP20** and **BB-LP30** gain only the explicitly documented range memberships, with neutral shared descriptions where necessary. Their IDs, model numbers, primary documents and known weights remain unchanged. HP-R501 is a new shared 1⅛-inch threaded-headset identity; HP-R500's mass is not transferred to it.

The new memberships do not replace existing weighed references. **PD-M350** keeps its existing **445 g pair** reference, and HP-R500 keeps its existing **121 g** selected reference. Neither mass is transferred to a neighbouring model.

BB-UN51 retains its unknown mass and existing 110/113 mm options, with the separately documented 118 mm option added. No assertion equates 110 with 110.5 mm. BB-LP20 keeps the existing **341 g / 113 mm** reference from the 1994 dealer review; this pass does not replace its weighed spindle. BB-LP30 remains unknown. Hub figures do not establish skewer exclusion and are not used or reduced by an estimated skewer mass.

## Further review

This is a focused Exage ES/LT review, not a complete audit of all 79 STI pages. Altus A10/A20/C10/C20, the other LX service configurations, 105 SC and the additional-product sheets remain further review material. Similar model codes in other dated families need an identity check before sharing or replacing existing records. Hybrid 700CX/400CX systems are not forced into the available road/MTB/gravel/track categories.

The [27 November Altus follow-up](shimano-1993-altus.md) adds the separately named A10/A20/C10/C20 ranges and their documented inventories, while preserving these Exage records.
