# Sword Black and ADVENT MX catalogue expansion

Reviewed on **22 November 2026** using original manufacturer publications and manufacturer product/store specifications. The [2025 catalogue](https://www.microshift.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Microshift-2025-Catalog-20240911.pdf) and newly downloaded [2026 catalogue](https://www.microshift.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/2026_-Microshift_Catalog_261105.pdf) were checked visually against their exact model, configuration and weight columns. The 2026 PDF has 47 spreads; its content and model-specific tables take precedence over the text extraction's repeated decorative headers.

This batch adds **three groupsets, 21 components and 13 sourced weights**, and links four existing cassette identities to their explicitly documented Sword Black range. No existing component weight, identity or primary technical-document URL changes. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, 267 groupsets and 1,817 components.

Compatibility with ADVENT X or Sword alone does not create membership in those older ranges.

The clamp front-derailleur table prints 131 g without identifying its weighed band diameter; that figure remains a source note. Crank tables identify lengths, rings and a compatible 68×113 mm bottom bracket, but do not explicitly establish bottom-bracket exclusion from the published weight. Rear-derailleur smallest-sprocket and maximum front-difference limits remain null where the reviewed source does not specify them. A minimum permitted **largest** sprocket is stored separately and is never mistaken for the smallest sprocket.
