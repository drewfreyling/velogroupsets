# STX MC32, Altus C90 and complete control-set weights

Reviewed on **22 November 2026** from original manufacturer dealer manuals. The **1995** [STX product spread on pages 24–25](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1995_Shimano_Dealer_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=24) explicitly introduces the updated STX with IG drivetrain and MC32 components. The [Altus C90 spread on pages 30–31](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1995_Shimano_Dealer_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=30) names that range and illustrates its six-/seven-speed variants. Both are classified by the manufacturer as MTB ranges.

This batch adds **two groupsets, 34 component records and nine sourced weights**: eight weights on new records and the previously unknown SL-M660 pair figure. It also adds explicit shared memberships and clarifies three pedal descriptions. Existing canonical IDs, primary technical documents and previously known weights are preserved. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, 270 groupsets and 1,877 components.

## Range dates and inventories

Both new ranges use **1995 documented availability**, rather than claiming a precise launch date. STX MC32 remains separate from the earlier MC30 and Special Edition entries and the STX-RC range.

| Range | Manufacturer table | Added models |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STX MC32 | 1995 p. 32 | RD-MC32, FD-MC32, ST-MC32, ST-MC35, BR-MC32, FC-MC32, BB-UN51, FH-MC32, HB-MC32, CS-IG60 |
| Altus C90 | 1995 p. 33 | RD-CT90-GS, RD-CT90-SS, FD-CT90-E, ST-CT90-7S, ST-CT95-7S, ST-CT90-6S, SB-CT90-7S, SB-CT90-6S, BR-CT90, FC-CT90, BB-CT90, FH-CT90-7S, FH-CT90-6S, HB-CT90, CS-HG30-7, CS-HG30-6 |

The STX table also explicitly lists HP-MC30, HP-MC31 and PD-M323. Those existing records gain STX MC32 membership instead of being duplicated. PD-M323 is also listed in the Alivio column and gains that documented MC12 association. Earlier weight selections and the unresolved HP-MC31 thread discrepancy remain unchanged.

The seven-speed STX crank reference is 170 mm with 42/32/22T; Altus C90 uses a selected 170 mm 38/32/24T reference. BB-UN51 offers 110/113 mm spindles and BB-CT90 offers 116/121 mm. The tables do not publish qualified weights for these assemblies. Available configurations are recorded without inventing a mass.

Altus C90's front model is retained exactly as **FD-CT90-E**. The table states Easy Set Adapter and clamp options; page 31 explicitly says the Easy-Set design mounts the derailleur directly to the bottom bracket. That feature text establishes the mounting type, rather than the E suffix alone. The detailed specification table establishes BB-CT90's identity; the product spread's inconsistent caption above its bottom-bracket photograph is not used as an alias to BR-CT90.

## Complete shifter-set references

The [2006 manual, page 184](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2006_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=184) and [page 185](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2006_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=185) identify a **Shift Lever Set**. Each exact model column specifies **both triple front and nine-speed rear controls** and distinguishes standalone SL sets from combined ST shift/brake sets. The published assembly figure is therefore used for the complete left/right set. An individual-side figure is never doubled and an ST total is never transferred to SL.

| Model | Complete reference | Weight | Source page | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ST-M961 | XTR 3×9 combined V-brake/mechanical-disc control pair | 412 g | 2006, 184 | Added |
| ST-M761 | Deore XT 3×9 combined V-brake/mechanical-disc control pair | 440 g | 2006, 184 | Added |
| SL-M952 | XTR 3×9 standalone shifter pair | 252 g | 2006, 184 | Added |
| ST-M580 | Deore LX 3×9 combined V-brake/mechanical-disc control pair | 430 g | 2006, 185 | Added |
| ST-M530 | Deore 3×9 combined V-brake/mechanical-disc control pair | 450 g | 2006, 185 | Added |
| SL-M571 | Deore LX 3×9 standalone shifter pair | 240 g | 2006, 185 | Added |
| SL-M660 | SLX 3×9 standalone shifter pair | 256 g | 2009, 207 | Existing null filled |

The [2009 table on page 207](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2009_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=207) establishes the same complete-set basis for SL-M660. Reading the assembly heading and both front/rear rows resolves the earlier source lead. Its 256 g figure is separate from the BL-M665 hydraulic brake-lever figure below it. Cable/housing inclusion is not specified for these set weights and remains a stated limitation.

These are manufacturer complete-set totals. They are not calculated side-weight sums and do not use the calculated-weight method label. The source basis is consistent with the earlier [1997–2000 control review](shimano-mtb-controls-1997-2000.md), which also uses explicitly defined complete front/rear lever sets.

## Other qualified weights

| Model | Reference | Weight | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FD-M961 | M-band clamp front derailleur, 31.8 mm | 149 g | 2006 pp. 81, 182 |
| PD-M959 | Complete left/right XTR SPD pedal pair | 350 g | 2006 pp. 162, 189; manufacturer XTR classification |

FD-M961's weight is explicitly marked **M** in the specification table. The front-derailleur feature text identifies the medium clamp as **31.8 mm** and discusses its 28.6 mm adapter separately. This supplies the actual band size rather than selecting one arbitrarily from the options.

PD-M959's specification row explicitly says **pair**. The [manufacturer's discontinued-products index](https://bike.shimano.com/en-AU/products/discontinued.html) identifies it as XTR, and the 2006 dealer manual documents it during the M960 XTR range. The membership represents this catalogue-era association, not a claim that the pedal was exclusive to M960 or introduced with it. Cleat inclusion is unspecified.

## Existing pedal descriptions

PD-M747 and PD-M535 now use the neutral description “Dual-sided SPD pedal pair”; PD-M636 uses “SPD platform pedal pair”. Their documented period table memberships are preserved without presenting those associations as exclusive branding. The [1997 manual, page 35](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1997_Shimano_Dealer_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=35) explicitly markets PD-M636 for BMX, alongside its presence in the earlier Deore XT specification column. No BMX groupset or category is invented in this batch.

## Remaining gaps

The lower-tier 1995 tables are strong inventory and gearing-limit evidence but largely omit weights. Cassettes retain the printed speed, letter group and tooth range; hubs require a configuration-specific skewer-free reference; brake records retain unknown front/rear-pair weights until pads, bolts and the assembly unit are established. Missing data stays null. Other STX revisions, generic MTB pedal associations and hybrid/BMX categories still need separate source/model review.
