# Shimano 2016 USA catalogue: Tiagra and road references

Reviewed on **24 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 2016 Bicycle Components USA catalogue](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/2016_Bicycle_Components_usa.pdf). The PDF has **264 pages**. PDF and printed page numbers match in the reviewed road sections. This is the product catalogue, not the separate 2016 STI or original-parts manual.

This pass adds **ten components and three sourced weights**, enriches **five existing records** and expands **Tiagra 4700 from ten to 17 components**. No groupsets are added. Following the [1990 Exage LX review](shimano-1990-exage-lx.md) and this batch, the catalogue contains four manufacturers, **277 groupsets and 2,099 components**. Existing positive weights, canonical IDs, model numbers and primary documents are preserved; RD-4700-GS receives a sourced correction for its selected double configuration.

## Existing configurations and conditional RD-4700-GS capacity

The same pages fill the following facts without assigning new masses:

- **ST-4700**: double-front 2×10 configuration.
- **FD-4700-F**: two front speeds and the FD-4700 family's directly printed 16T capacity; its existing braze-on identity is preserved.
- **FC-4700**: double-front configuration, 50/34T and 52/36T options, excluding the separately listed bottom bracket; no weighed arm length is inferred.
- **CS-HG500-10**: available 11–25T, 12–28T, 11–32T and 11–34T options; no selected weight or missing tooth sequence is invented.

Page 161 prints **“Total capacity: 33T (SS), 39T (2x), 41T (3x)”**. The existing RD-4700-GS record selects a double configuration, so its canonical capacity is corrected from the later handbook's unsplit **41T maximum** to the explicitly scoped **39T double reference**. The **41T triple value** is retained separately in `total_capacity_triple_teeth`, and the record explains the distinction and keeps both source citations.

Its existing manufacturer-handbook double limits—34T largest sprocket, 11T smallest sprocket and 16T front difference—remain unchanged. Capacity is transcribed from the 2016 double line, not calculated from those three fields. The earlier generic 41T entry is not claimed to be an explicit double-only rating or discarded as a universally wrong figure.

## Figures not transferred

The Dura-Ace FC-9000 and Ultegra FC-6800 figures in the selected product pages **include bottom brackets**. They are not published as standalone crank weights, and a BB mass is not subtracted. FC-5800's 725 g / 50–34T figure explicitly excludes the BB but does **not identify the weighed arm length**, so it does not replace the existing qualified 170 mm crank reference.

The selected hub rows do not establish skewer exclusion and do not supply new canonical hub masses. Caliper figures and images are not used to assume every required pad/bolt inclusion. The Tiagra pages publish useful configurations but no qualifying new Tiagra masses.

This is a focused road/Tiagra review, not a complete audit of all 264 pages. MTB, remaining Di2 inventory, disc-brake assemblies, trekking/utility ranges and other catalogue sections remain further review material. Unsupported range categories are not inferred from compatibility alone.
