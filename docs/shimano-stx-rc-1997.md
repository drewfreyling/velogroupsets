# STX-RC range and 1997 MTB weight follow-up

Reviewed on **22 November 2026** from original manufacturer-hosted manuals. The [1995 dealer sales/support manual, PDF/printed page 4](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1995_Shimano_Dealer_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=4) explicitly introduces STX-RC as an all-new high-performance MTB group. Its start year is therefore **1995**. The range stays separate from the earlier STX and STX Special Edition records.

The component inventory uses the [1997 manual, pages 30–34](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1997_Shimano_Dealer_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=30). That edition lists seven- and eight-speed options under one STX-RC heading. They are represented by one range with model/configuration label **7/8-speed**, while each control, rear derailleur and rear hub retains its actual speed. Later parts are not backdated to the group's introduction.

This batch adds **one groupset, 26 component records and eleven newly sourced weights**: ten weights on new records and the previously unknown HP-MC30 figure. Three existing shared components gain STX-RC membership. Existing IDs, primary technical-document URLs and previously known weights are preserved. The catalogue now contains 268 groupsets and 1,843 components across four manufacturers.

## Withheld figures and discrepancies

- FC-MC36-S and FC-MC36 have 759 g / 170 mm figures and identified 42/32/22T chainrings, but the table does not explicitly establish bottom-bracket exclusion. No BB mass is subtracted.
- FD-MC36's 135 g figure does not identify which available clamp band was weighed. The E-type figure is not inferred from it.
- The brake table's 366 g BR-MC33-E reference does not establish whether the weighed assembly covers one wheel's arms or a complete front/rear set. It remains a source lead, not a canonical pair weight.
- Hub figures such as HB-MC33 212 g and FH-MC33 432 g do not establish the project's skewer-free convention. They remain unknown; no deduction is made.
- HP-MC31 is listed at 190 g with a printed 24 TPI thread in 1997. The [1994 manufacturer drawing, page 27](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1994_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=27), explicitly specifies 26 TPI for HP-MC31. The earlier standard is preserved; weight stays null until the reference is resolved. No undocumented hardware revision is invented.
- Existing known headset weights, including HP-M740's selected 1994 reference, are not overwritten by slightly different later table figures.

The 1995 manual also identifies 700CX and 400CX as hybrid ranges. They are research leads requiring category and component review, rather than being silently classified as road or MTB. This is a selected-source follow-up, not an exhaustive audit of either manual.
