# Shimano 1998 STI Ultegra review

Reviewed on **23 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 1998 Shimano STI manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1998_Shimano_STI.pdf). The archive disclaimer identifies the information as updated in 1998. The PDF has **144 pages**, including Ultegra specifications and double/triple service sheets. PDF and printed page numbers differ; citations record both and URL fragments use PDF page numbers.

This targeted pass adds **three components and one manufacturer-listed weight**, and enriches **four existing component records**. Existing IDs, start years, primary technical documents and known masses are preserved. The catalogue contains four manufacturers, 272 groupsets and 1,976 components after the batch.

The [FD-6503 service sheet](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1998_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=85) gives a small-band range of **28.0–28.6 mm** alongside the braze-on option. Those available mounts do not establish an actual weighed clamp or an assembly mass. The overview's **81 g** figure explicitly says **double, braze-on**, so it remains associated with the existing FD-6500-F reference and is not copied into FD-6503.

The [RD-6500 service table](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1998_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=81) separates SS and GS columns. RD-6500-GS is recorded with **37T total capacity, 27T largest sprocket, 11T smallest sprocket and 22T maximum front difference**. These are the printed GS values, not derived from the SS model, crank rings or cage length.

The overview gives a **215 g** rear-derailleur figure under a combined SS/GS heading without identifying its weighed cage. The new GS mass therefore remains unknown. The existing SS identity, limits and selected 215 g weight stay unchanged.

## BB-6500 weight reference and its limit

The [Ultegra specification page, PDF 59 / printed 58](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1998_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=59), explicitly states **“221.5 g (68 mm)”** for BB-6500 and identifies a splined crank mount. The [service table, PDF 87 / printed 86](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1998_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=87), separately lists **109.5 mm for the double** and **118.5 mm for the triple**.

The canonical figure is the manufacturer's stated **68 mm-shell reference**. Its description explicitly says that the weight entry does **not** identify the spindle variant. Neither available spindle length is silently selected, and the record does not claim that both variants necessarily weigh the same. This is a splined assembly: the weight guidelines require its interface to be identified, while the mandatory weighed spindle-length rule applies to square-taper references.

A more precise manufacturer reference could later identify the weighed spindle. The available 109.5/118.5 mm options are preserved separately from the known shell width and are not presented as an actual measured spindle length. No component mass is calculated by subtracting a crank or bottom-bracket figure.

## Existing configuration details

- **FD-6500-F:** gains the explicit double-front configuration, nine rear speeds, **15T front capacity** and **43.5 mm chainline** from PDF 83 / printed 82. Its existing braze-on mount and 81 g reference remain intact.
- **ST-6500:** gains explicit available front speeds of **two and three** from the named double/triple STI table on PDF 77 / printed 76. No fictitious ST-6503 model is added.
- **FC-6500:** gains available **165/167.5/170/172.5/175 mm** arm lengths and a **130 mm** bolt circle from PDF 87 / printed 86. Its known 175 mm, 53/39T weighed reference is preserved.
- **FC-6503:** gains the same documented arm options and **130/74 mm** bolt circles from that exact triple column. Its known 172.5 mm, 52/42/30T weighed reference is preserved rather than converted to an estimated 170 mm mass.
