# Shimano Exage road groupsets

The catalogue includes Exage Sport, Exage Motion, 300EX, 400EX and the previously added 500EX. These records cover documented road equipment, with one identity for parts Shimano lists in multiple groupsets. Exage Action, Sport LX, mountain-bike LX groups and later ES ranges are separate offerings and are not added by this change.

[Shimano’s Exage history](https://www.shimano.com/en/100th/history/products/result.html?id=40) identifies Sport in 1987 and the EX range in 1989. It does not separately date Motion. Motion’s start is therefore the documented August 1988 brochure listing, rather than an inferred launch year for the entire Exage family. The 1990 dealer manual was published in August 1989: its cover model year is not substituted for the publication date. Only the documented start year is recorded; end years are not collected.

The principal component sources are original Shimano publications preserved by Disraeli Gears:

- *The New System Component Family for Every Riding Style*, August 1988: [page 1](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/shimano_-_the_new_system_component_family_for_every_riding_style_-_page_1.html), [page 9, road drivetrains and controls](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/shimano_-_the_new_system_component_family_for_every_riding_style_-_page_9.html) and [page 10, shared equipment and options](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/shimano_-_the_new_system_component_family_for_every_riding_style_-_page_10.html).

## Components and shared parts

| Groupset | Listed components | Principal distinct parts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exage Sport | 18 | RD-A450, FC-A450/FC-A452, FD-A450/FD-A451, SL-A450/SL-A451/SL-A452, HB-A450-F, FH-A450-6 |
| Exage Motion | 13 | RD-A250, FC-A250, FD-A250, SL-A250 flipper levers, HB-RA50-F, FH-RM50 |
| Exage 400EX | 11 | RD-A400, FC-A400, FD-A400-B; SL-A400-FCAI shared with 300EX |
| Exage 300EX | 11 | RD-A300, FC-A300, FD-A300-B; SL-A400-FCAI shared with 400EX |
| Exage 500EX | 11 | Existing component list; see [the 500EX source notes](catalogue.md#historical-shimano-exage-500ex) |

The five Exage road groupsets contain **44 unique component identities across 64 memberships**. Sport and Motion share BB-A450, the six-speed CS-1000 cassette, PD-A450 pedals and HP-A450 headset. Motion can also use SL-A452 downtube controls instead of SL-A250 handlebar-mounted flipper levers. The Sport record includes original SL-A450/FD-A450 equipment documented by Shimano service instructions and revised controls shown in the 1988 brochure.

The 1988 brochure distinguishes front SIS from non-front-SIS alternatives. FC-A450 is front-SIS compatible; FC-A452 is the listed non-front-SIS crank option. SL-A451 provides front SIS, while SL-A452 does not. Group membership is not a claim that every listed control, derailleur and crank variant is interchangeable.

Both numbered EX specification pages explicitly refer to 500EX for the shared FH-HG50 rear freehub, HB-RM50 front hub, CS-HG50 cassette, PD-A550 pedals and HP-R500 headset. BB-A450 is also listed directly. Their existing identities and weight-reference configurations are preserved. The earlier Motion front hub is **HB-RA50**, not HB-RM50; the older Uniglide freehubs are distinct from FH-HG50. These names have not been normalised into a single part.

Sport and Motion are documented with six-speed Uniglide drivetrains. The 300EX and 400EX records describe seven-speed Hyperglide equipment, with the rear derailleurs also listed as six-speed SIS compatible. Their rear-derailleur limits are 28T total capacity, 28T largest sprocket, 12T smallest sprocket and 13T maximum front difference. RD-A450 and RD-A250 instead specify a 13T smallest sprocket. No SS/GS cage code is inferred for these models.

| BL-A351 | 302 g | Pair of aero brake levers, 23.8–24.2 mm handlebar clamp; 400EX |
| BL-A500 | 258 g | Pair of aero brake levers, 23.8–24.2 mm handlebar clamp; 500EX |
| BL-A251 | 310 g | Pair of standard aero brake levers, 23.8–24.2 mm handlebar clamp; 300EX and Motion |

The earlier Sport/Motion parts remain listed with null weights until a compliant reference is recorded. In particular, a shifter weight must cover a pair and a crank weight must identify arm length, chainrings and bottom-bracket exclusion. Hub weights must exclude skewers; no old catalogue figure has been reduced by an estimated skewer weight.

BB-A450 retains its existing 333 g reference for a 113 mm spindle listed with FC-A500. The 400EX page prints 333.3 g, while the 300EX page prints 333 g; these have not been averaged or presented as a weight for every spindle/thread variant. Shared cassette records retain their own six- or seven-speed tooth configurations. As in the contribution policy, cassette lockring inclusion is assumed without further verification.

This is documented group coverage, not every revision or option. Additional band/braze-on variants, freewheel-equipped rear hubs and later revisions remain to be added. Seatposts are not asserted where the cited line-ups do not list them. Standalone brake levers now include BL-A351 for 400EX, BL-A450/BL-A451/BL-A452/BL-A453 for Sport and the separate BL-A251-EX Motion option; see the [source notes and remaining gaps](brake-levers.md). Brake calipers, chains and screw-on freewheels remain outside the current taxonomy; their omission is not a claim that the original groups lacked them.

BL-A251 is listed in both the 1988 Motion line-up and the 1989 300EX specification table, so it has one identity with both memberships. Its 310 g reference comes from the 300EX table. The separately named BL-A251-EX model has auxiliary extension levers and is not represented by this standard-lever weight. BL-A500 uses the Super SLR pairing specified with BR-A500 and Shimano outer cable; BL-A251 is listed for SLR-compatible brakes with lined Shimano outer cable.
