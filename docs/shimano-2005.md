# Shimano 2005 Trade Sales Support Manual

The [manufacturer-hosted handbook](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2005_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf) provides road component features, group line-ups and detailed specification tables. This review fills 16 previously unknown weights and adds the later BR-5501 caliper pair. It does not claim a complete audit of the handbook’s mountain-bike, comfort or accessory ranges.

The relevant road/specification pages have printed numbers **12 higher than the PDF page index**. For example, BR-6600’s overview is PDF page 123 / printed page 135, and its detailed F&R table is PDF page 176 / printed page 188. Component citations identify both numbers and link to the PDF page.

The front hub table explicitly labels its row **“Average Weight w/o QR (QR weight)”** on the left page; the same row continues across the facing page. The rear hub table uses the same convention. The parenthetical QR figure is a separate skewer weight. FH-7800’s overview gives 328 g, but the detailed table specifies **264 (64) g** without QR: the catalogue takes 264 g directly from that row, without deriving it by subtraction. The manual lists several drilling options without identifying which was weighed, so hub descriptions retain that limitation.

Caliper tables explicitly label the figures **F&R** and identify standard pads; the assembly references include fixing hardware. The weighed mounting-nut/bolt variant is unspecified. BR-5501 remains a separate identity from BR-5500: 353 g is not substituted for the earlier model’s 352 g reference. BR-A550-57’s 397 g is a later 2005 reference with R50T pads, separate from the original 49-type 348 g listing.

## Group membership and corroborating references

The Tiagra, Sora and 2200 line-ups on PDF pages 131, 133 and 135 explicitly list BR-A550-57 as a long-reach option. The 2200 line-up also lists BR-3300. These are shared component identities, rather than duplicated calipers or inferred membership from cable compatibility. The existing RX100 membership of BR-A550-57 remains intact.

The manual also corroborates existing ST-6600 **490 g/pair**, HB-6600 **140 g without QR**, FH-6600 **347 g without QR**, SP-6600 **214 g**, CS-6600 **235 g for ten-speed 12–23T** and CS-7800 **173 g for ten-speed 11–23T**. Their records gain this accessible manufacturer reference without changing those weights.

## Figures not used

- FC-7800, FC-6600 and FC-6603 totals explicitly include the integrated bottom bracket. They do not qualify as crankset-only weights; no bearing mass is estimated or subtracted.
- ST-7703 and ST-6603 have front-lever-only figures in the selected table. These are not doubled into pair weights. The SL-7800 / SL-BS78 rows do not explicitly establish a pair basis, so their weights remain unknown.
- FD-7703 lists distinct band and braze-on references. The [front-derailleur review](shimano-front-derailleur-weights.md) now explicitly selects the **103 g FD-7703-F braze-on** reference on the existing model record. The separate 113 g band figure is not applied to that configuration.
- The 2005 BR-7700 reference is 318 g with R55C+1 pads. The existing 317.5 g reference is from the 2001 table with R55C pads. The source/configuration difference is preserved; the figures are not averaged.
