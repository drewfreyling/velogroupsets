# Early Deore XT and Deore: the 1987 catalogue

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 from the supplied [Shimano 1987 complete-line catalogue scan](https://vintagecannondale.com/cannondale/shimano/1987%20Shimano.pdf). The publication has 28 PDF pages; the final page states copyright **December 1986**, with print code `1286 FC/100M`. References below use PDF page numbers. The archive filename is not used to infer component introduction years.

This selected MTB review adds **four control records** and **six newly sourced weights**: four weights on existing derailleur records and two on new brake-lever pairs. The catalogue now contains 1,447 components. Existing IDs, slugs, model numbers, memberships and primary technical documents are preserved.

## Weights established

| Model | Weight | Exact reference | PDF page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RD-M730 | 265 g | Complete Deore XT rear derailleur | 22 |
| RD-MT60 | 311 g | Complete Deore rear derailleur | 24 |
| FD-M730 | 112 g | Normal-band clamp fitting 28.0–28.6 mm, not the 31.8 mm EC clamp | 22 |
| FD-MT60 | 113 g | Normal-band clamp fitting 28.0–28.6 mm, not the 31.8 mm EC clamp | 24 |
| BL-M730 | 317 g | Complete left/right brake-lever pair, 22.2 mm handlebar clamps | 23 |
| BL-MT60 | 300 g | Complete left/right brake-lever pair, 22.2 mm handlebar clamps | 24 |

The front-derailleur tables identify the actual normal band's **28.0–28.6 mm fitting range** and list the separate 31.8 mm EC endless clamp. The canonical records identify `mount: "Clamp"`, `clamp_diameter_range_mm: [28, 28.6]` and the normal-band reference. They do not assign the weight to an arbitrary selection from different clamp sizes. The weighed AL/HS guide option is not identified; that source limitation remains explicit rather than inventing a variant-specific figure.

Both brake-lever weights are explicitly marked **Pair**. Their source does not establish cable/housing inclusion, so that remains an explicit limitation. Manufacturer-hosted primary technical documents identify the actual assemblies:

- [BL-M730 exploded view](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/BL-M730-0779/EV-BL-M730-0779A.pdf).
- [BL-MT60 exploded view](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/BL-MT60-0792/EV-BL-MT60-0792A.pdf).

The catalogue also explicitly lists **PD-M730 at 412 g per pair** on PDF page 22, corroborating the existing manufacturer reference. Its weight is unchanged; the additional source citation is retained.

## Rear-derailleur limits and fork ends

| Model | Total capacity | Largest sprocket | Smallest sprocket | Separate maximum front difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RD-M730 | 38T | 28–32T, with source's fork-end condition | 12T | Not stated |
| RD-MT60 | 38T | 28–32T, with source's fork-end condition | 13T | Not stated |

The source associates the **28–32T largest-sprocket range with Shimano SFR/SF fork ends**. The canonical maximum is 32T with that condition recorded in both specifications and the weight description; it is not presented as an unconditional limit for every frame. The shared cage-help component remains unchanged, and no cage suffix is inferred from capacity or appearance.

“Maximum Front & Rear Sprocket Difference” supplies the total capacity, not a separate front-chainring limit. The latter remains null. The 12T and 13T smallest-sprocket limits belong to different models and are not copied between them.

## Controls added without a verified pair weight

- **SL-M730:** complete left/right thumb-shifter pair, 22.2 mm clamp, SIS/friction switching. The catalogue lists 191 g without explicitly identifying the weight as a pair; its canonical weight stays null. [Manufacturer assembly drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/SL-M730-0775/EV-SL-M730-0775B.pdf).
- **SL-MT60:** complete left/right thumb-shifter pair, 22.2 mm clamp, SIS/friction switching. The 210 g entry likewise lacks an explicit pair basis and is not doubled or published as the pair total. [Manufacturer assembly drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/SL-MT60-0784/EV-SL-MT60-0784B.pdf).

The photographed front/rear controls establish the pair inventory, but a photograph alone is not used to resolve whether the printed mass covers both controls. The nearby SL-MS55 and SL-MS40 entries explicitly say Pair; their figures are different models and are not transferred.

The 1987 FC-M730 illustration also describes an earlier Biopace II assembly. Its mass is not transferred to the existing later Round Gear SG-X record or the separately sourced 1991 SG reference. Hub figures still require explicit skewer exclusion. This is a checked batch of selected early MTB pages, not an audit of all 28 pages. Supporting archive citations do not create a new primary-document archive exception, and no source images or PDFs are committed.
