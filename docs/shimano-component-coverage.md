# Shimano component coverage audit

Reviewed on **15 November 2026**. The audit found **57 of 138 Shimano groupsets with no components**. This update adds **141 manufacturer-documented component identities**, so every existing Shimano groupset now has at least one component. It preserves all groupset IDs and start years. The full catalogue now has **1,118 components across 238 groupsets and four manufacturers**.

These are sourced starter inventories, not complete build kits. Five of the previously empty ranges have one verified component, 20 have two, and 32 have three. Remaining categories and most weights still need reference review.

## What was added

| Type | New records |
| --- | --- |
| Rear derailleurs | 54 |
| Front derailleurs | 45 |
| Cranksets | 39 |
| Complete shifter pairs | 2 |
| Front hubs | 1 |

Each component links to its exact manufacturer-hosted exploded drawing. Family/generation labels and period catalogue rows establish associations; models are not created by adding prefixes to a groupset number. Discovery queries that lacked an exact assembly drawing were discarded. The [catalogue guide](catalogue.md) and [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md) continue to apply.

## Sources and identity decisions

- **Original Dura-Ace:** the manufacturer drawing identifies the HB-7110-F front hub. The original [catalogue archived as 1981, printed p. 29 / PDF p. 30](https://www.goatsurfer.com/assets/Shimano_1981.pdf#page=30) places HB-7110 in the conventional Dura-Ace line, separately from EX and AX. This is a later reference within that line, not a claim that this hub was part of the 1973 launch kit. Its skewer-free weight remains null.
- **Original 600 and road Altus:** manufacturer drawings identify FD-6100, RD-AT12 and RD-AT30. The same original catalogue separately identifies the [600 ensemble, printed p. 31 / PDF p. 32](https://www.goatsurfer.com/assets/Shimano_1981.pdf#page=32) and [Altus/Selecta, printed p. 38 / PDF p. 39](https://www.goatsurfer.com/assets/Shimano_1981.pdf#page=39). Later MTB Altus A20/CT/M parts are not transferred into those road groups. The initially considered FC-AT20 was rejected because its manufacturer drawing identifies the later Altus A20 assembly.
- **Acera M300:** the [2000 dealer manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2000_Shimano_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=56) supplies the dated pre-M340 reference for M330-series Acera assemblies. Its range history remains the existing 1998 reference; the component source edition is not treated as a new launch date.
- **STX Special Edition:** the [1994 manual, p. 26](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1994_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=26) identifies the Chromica assemblies. The CH finish and CHT top-route variant are retained; they are not merged with the standard MC30 group.
- **SLX and Deore:** Shimano identifies RD-M662 with M660, and RD-M4120 with the M4100 reference. No RD-M660 or RD-M4100 is invented.
- **Crank variants:** FC-M570-4, FC-M739-4 and FC-M750-4 retain the separately documented four-arm identities. Missing generic crank codes were not substituted with a similarly numbered part.
- **Service-index tags:** the drawing itself is authoritative when index tags disagree. For example, the RD-M810 drawing explicitly says SAINT, and the FD-M360 drawing says ACERA. Period line-up citations also confirm generic-labelled FC-MC20 / FC-M340 cranksets and the Deore FD-M530.
- **GRX and MTB Di2:** the import adds the documented controls, derailleurs and crank assemblies for these ranges. Mechanical components are not attached to Di2 ranges solely because they appear compatible.

The original archived catalogue is a supplementary membership/weight citation. Every primary technical-document URL remains on Shimano’s own service domain; no archive exception was added.

The other **138 new weights remain null**. A service drawing proves identity and parts construction, not a configuration-specific mass. Hub masses including quick-release skewers and individual control weights are not converted into compliant values. The two shifter records retain `weight_basis: "Pair"` even while their weights are unknown.

## Derailleur limits

The reviewed service instructions add **54 limit fields across 14 rear derailleurs**. RD-M662, RD-M760, RD-M580, RD-M770, RD-M600, RD-M591, RD-M410, RD-M360, RD-M780, RD-M980 and RD-M960 select the explicitly documented SGS table reference. RD-M310 and RD-M340 use their Smart Cage references. Cage options are not blended to combine the greatest values from different configurations. The individual records link to the exact SI document.

RD-AT12 separately records its 13T front difference and general 28T largest sprocket, with the manufacturer’s conditional Shimano-only 30T allowance preserved as a separate specification. Its total capacity and smallest sprocket remain null. Other limits not established by the selected drawing/manual remain null.

## Preventing empty additions

A public API regression check now requires every Shimano range to have a component association. New Shimano range additions should include at least one verified component rather than publishing an empty groupset record. Browser tests exercise historical, MTB and GRX navigation, manufacturer technical links and the static component API.
