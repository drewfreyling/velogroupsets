# Standalone brake levers

Standalone brake levers have their own **Brake Levers** category. Integrated ST and Ergopower shift/brake controls remain **Shifters**; they are not duplicated here. Known weights describe a left/right pair under the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md#standalone-brake-levers).

Each component record links to the exact source page and to a Shimano-hosted technical document or the [approved Campagnolo archive](campagnolo-record.md#approved-archived-manufacturer-documents). Original Shimano catalogues preserved by Disraeli Gears establish membership and specifications; they do not replace the required manufacturer-hosted technical document.

The 2007 line-up explicitly includes BL-R600 as a separate-control option alongside those three groupsets. It has one shared identity, rather than three renamed series components. BL-22RECG likewise has one identity for the contemporary Record offerings. Membership is evidence of a listed option, not a claim that every control and brake combination is interchangeable.

## Weights and variants

The period catalogues explicitly establish pair weights for BL-A351 (302 g), BL-A500 (258 g), BL-A251 (310 g), BL-A550 (252 g), BL-1050 (202 g), BL-1051 (252 g), BL-1055 (252 g), BL-6401 (260 g), BL-7400 (216 g) and BL-7402 (240 g). The BL-TT79 average is 89 g per pair in Shimano’s April 2026 specification edition; its 7900 membership comes separately from the 2011 line-up. Each record identifies the weighed model and the source edition.

Other added lever weights remain null. In particular:

- BL-7300 is listed at 107 g each with pad in the [archived 1982 catalogue](https://labibleduvelocataloguesshimano.blogspot.com/p/shimano-1982.html), printed page 16. It remains null because an individual lever weight is not a pair weight.
- BL-A251-EX has auxiliary extension levers. The standard BL-A251 pair weight does not describe this assembly.
- BL-1052 and BL-6402 are short-reach models; neighbouring standard-reach weights are not substituted.
- BL-A410’s RSX weight column is blank. Adjacent RX100 figures are not RSX weights.
- The cited BL-6403 and BL-7401 entries do not explicitly identify a pair weight, so their figures are withheld pending a compliant reference.

## Remaining coverage gaps

This is sourced coverage of the listed models, rather than every standalone lever or revision ever offered. Golden Arrow BL-A105 and later 105 SC BL-1056 need a qualifying manufacturer-hosted technical document before addition. BL-TT78, other time-trial options and further non-series lever memberships need their own group-specific evidence; compatibility alone does not establish membership. The shared BL-R600 memberships above are limited to the cited 2007 line-up, not inferred for every Ultegra, 105 or Dura-Ace generation.

No standalone lever is assigned to the Light Action rear-derailleur family or the track groups merely to fill a category. Original 1973 Dura-Ace component documentation also remains a gap. Modern integrated controls already represented as Shifters do not require a second brake-only record. Brake calipers remain outside the current taxonomy.
