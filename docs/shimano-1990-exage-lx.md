# Shimano 1990 Exage LX band and GS references

Reviewed on **24 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 1990 Dealer Product Manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1990_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf). The PDF has **68 pages**. This follow-up reviews **PDF 55 / printed 52** (500LX), **PDF 56 / printed 53** (400LX) and **PDF 57 / printed 54** (300LX), alongside the earlier [XT/DX/LX review](shimano-mtb-1990.md).

This pass adds **four component references and five newly sourced weights**, enriches **three existing records**, and moves the documented availability of all three Exage LX ranges from 1991 to **1990**. No groupsets are added. After this batch the catalogue contains four manufacturers, 277 groupsets and **2,089 components**. The dates remain first reviewed availability references, not claimed launch years; the later 1991/1992 evidence is preserved.

## Five qualified weights

| Exact reference | Weight | PDF / printed page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FD-M500, normal band 28.0–28.6 mm | 118 g | 55 / 52 |
| FD-M400, normal band 28.0–28.6 mm | 132 g | 56 / 53 |
| FD-M300, normal band 28.0–28.6 mm | 135 g | 57 / 54 |
| RD-M400-GS, long cage | 318 g | 56 / 53 |
| CS-HG50 E, seven-speed 12–28T | 325 g | 55 / 52; explicitly shared by the 400LX/300LX notes |

The front tables explicitly associate their **normal-band** figures with a sole **28.0–28.6 mm band**. Larger 31.8/34.9 mm clamps are listed as EC-type alternatives. This qualifies the actual weighed band without choosing arbitrarily from several available sizes. All three records use `mount: "Clamp"` and `clamp_diameter_range_mm: [28, 28.6]`.

These are configuration-specific references with the **actual unsuffixed manufacturer model numbers**. Their IDs/slugs identify the normal-band 1990 reference; “normal-band-1990” is not invented as part of a model number. The existing unsuffixed EC-type and later -B band-type records keep their identities, configurations and unknown weights. No EC/-B/normal-band equivalence is asserted.

The RD-M400-GS row independently gives **318 g for long cage**. Its previously known 36T capacity / 30T largest / 12T smallest / 20T front difference are preserved. The 330 g SGS mass is not copied or modified, and the SS mass remains unknown.

The cassette's explicitly weighed **E group** is **12/14/16/18/21/24/28T**, seven sprockets, including spacers. Lockring inclusion follows the contribution convention. It is separate from the existing I 13–23T and M 13–28T CS-HG50 references. The manufacturer directly lists it with 500LX and explicitly states that 400LX/300LX share the 500LX cassette inventory; membership is not inferred merely from compatibility.

## Preserved reference distinctions

The selected **ST-M050 305 g pair** remains based on the explicitly paired [1991 400LX row](shimano-1991-dealer.md). The 1990 table labels its own 305 g figure **“4-Finger Type Rear”** and separately names a BL-M050 front-single lever. That earlier entry is not doubled, used to invent a left control, or used to redefine the selected 1991 pair. The source distinction is stated in the ST-M050 record.

The exact **BB-M500** receives explicitly documented Exage LX memberships. Its existing **339 g / 121.1 mm** weighed reference from the [1990 LX table](shimano-mtb-1990.md) is retained. The Exage rows separately print **121 mm**, so the record notes that distinction without asserting a dimensional alias or replacing the weighed spindle. This model is not renamed to the later BB-M550.

No existing positive mass, primary document, canonical ID or model number changes. No hub weights or estimated skewer deductions are added. Other groups and brake assembly definitions remain further review material; this is a focused follow-up rather than a complete import of all 68 pages.
