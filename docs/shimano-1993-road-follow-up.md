# Shimano 1993: further road identities and exact-spindle weights

Reviewed on **27 November 2026**, continuing after the [Altus A10/A20/C10/C20 pass](shimano-1993-altus.md). Sources are the original [1993 STI manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1993_Shimano_STI.pdf), the [matching dealer product manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1993_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manuall.pdf) and the exact Shimano exploded views linked below.

This follow-up adds **five components, three with qualified weights**, and fills two existing crank BCDs. Together with the preceding Altus pass, this work adds **four groupsets, 39 components and 11 qualified weights**. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, **281 groupsets and 2,248 components**. Existing IDs, model numbers, primary documents and positive weights remain unchanged.

## Missing 105 SC model identities

**FC-1056** is added with an unknown weight. STI PDF **52 / printed 50** explicitly prints its **130 mm BCD**, available arm lengths and 52/42T, 53/42T and 53/39T combinations. Dealer page **61** independently places the exact FC-1056 code in the 105 SC range. Neither page establishes a qualified crank-only mass for the selected model/configuration. The earlier FC-1055's 710 g reference is not copied or treated as an alias.

**FD-1056-F** is added as the manufacturer-labelled Italian braze-on A-type double-front assembly from dealer page **61**. Its mass remains unknown: the printed **105 g** is explicitly assigned to the **band-mounted** model. The existing FD-1056-B reference and its weighed band are preserved. The new record has the directly printed family capacity of 15T.

## Three BB-UN71 configurations

| Actual model | Spindle | Weight | Direct manufacturer reference | Membership |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BB-UN71 | 107 mm | 272 g | Dealer page 61, “BB-UN71 / 107 mm” | 105 SC 1055 |
| BB-UN71 | 115 mm | 272 g | Dealer page 55, “272 g (115 mm)” | 600 Ultegra 6400 |
| BB-UN71 | 122.5 mm | 280 g | Dealer page 15, “280 g (122.5 mm)” | Deore XT M730 |

All are square-taper bottom-bracket assembly references with the exact weighed spindle length. Each has a distinct configuration ID/slug while retaining the actual manufacturer model number **BB-UN71**. Other spindle lengths do not inherit these weights. Thread/shell alternatives are not presented as a uniquely identified weighed shell configuration, and additional memberships are not inferred solely from compatibility.

Bottom brackets remain separate from the crank weights. The broader application table on dealer page 76 identifies available configurations; it does not supply additional masses or justify extrapolating weights between lengths.

## Two further 130 mm BCD backfills

The exact [FC-1055 drawing, 1109](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/FC-1055-1109/EV-FC-1055-1109.pdf#page=1), explicitly prints **“Bolt circle diameter: 130mm”**. That dimension is added to the existing FC-1055 record without changing its weight or primary document.

The exact [FC-6400-SG drawing, 1150A](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/FC-6400-SG-1150/EV-FC-6400-SG-1150A.pdf#page=1), also explicitly prints **130 mm**. The existing FC-6400-SG retains its 650 g / 170 mm / 52/42T reference. The earlier FC-6400-BP identity is not renamed, aliased or assigned this fact without its own exact source review.

These follow the separate **FC-1050-BP 130 mm** correction, confirmed directly from its original Shimano drawing. BCD is not inferred from chainring teeth, appearance or another crank's compatibility.

## Limits of this pass

The remaining unqualified crank, hub, front-band, control and brake figures stay unknown. Published numbers are not transferred between different model codes, positional assemblies, bands, spindle lengths or generations. This is a further review of selected 1993 sections, not a claim that every remaining catalogue gap has been closed.
