# microSHIFT catalogue coverage

The first microSHIFT import was reviewed on **14 November 2026**, using the original manufacturer [2017 catalogue download](https://microshift.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/microSHIFT-catalogue-2017.pdf), also linked from its [official catalogue archive](https://www.microshift.com/pdf-catalog/). The uploaded copy was inspected locally; the canonical citations retain the original manufacturer URL. The PDF and page images are not redistributed in the repository.

The publication's index explicitly labels it **2016–2017**, and its opening pages say **2016 New Product Information**. Groupsets use **2016 as documented availability**, not as a claim that every family was first launched that year. The generation notes preserve that distinction. Printed page numbers are one less than PDF page numbers: printed p. 24 is PDF p. 25.

Coverage follows this edition. It does not claim to include the later ADVENT, ADVENT X, Acolyte or Sword families. City/utility and non-group parts without an explicit association to an imported range remain outside this pass. The catalogue does not establish microSHIFT cranksets, bottom brackets, hubs, calipers or other unlisted categories for these offerings.

## Source and variant decisions

Use the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md). These weights are manufacturer catalogue references, not contributor measurements.

- **Road rear derailleurs:** printed p. 24 supplies separate capacities, largest sprockets, 11T smallest sprockets and weights. A 25–32T range describes the allowed **largest** cassette sprocket, not the smallest. Front chainring difference stays null because the table does not specify it.
- **Mounts:** front-derailleur band and braze-on options have separate identities. The F/B suffix selects the actual mount-specific table weight; a braze-on mass is not copied to a clamp model. Clamp references identify 34.9 mm. The source's smaller diameters require optional reducers; they are not separate weighed clamp configurations. Nine road mount variants remain unknown. Product pages sometimes omit the extra hyphen used before F/B in the technical table; component records follow the product-page spelling and cite both pages.
- **Shifters:** all 30 records represent complete left/right pairs. Printed pp. 5–11 and 18–20 illustrate the pairs and identify the relevant configurations; the technical tables give the corresponding model averages. No individual control weights are doubled or added. The three MTB triple options SL-M860-3, SL-M850-3 and SL-M760-3 remain null because their numerical table entries are assigned to the double-chainring base models. SL-M861R is explicitly a single right-hand control and is not invented into a pair; it is omitted under the current pair-only shifter contract.
- **Cassettes:** the two CS-G110 configurations are explicitly shown with XCD 11, and the five CS-H100 configurations with XLE 10. Each range has its own record, exact sprocket sequence and supplied lockring. Merely sharing cassette spacing does not establish membership in other ranges. The 11–40T and 11–42T ten-speed sequences omit 19T; they are not copied from the eleven-speed versions.
- **Direct-mount front options:** this pass imports the documented band-mounted MTB versions. It does not relabel the separate direct-mount options as braze-on assemblies to fit the existing weight contract.
- **Compatibility:** Shimano compatibility remains a component specification. Every imported component belongs to microSHIFT, and no Shimano groupset memberships are added.

## Conflicting captions and table rows

The detailed technical table is the selected numerical reference where the same exact model has a different photo-caption figure. Both are manufacturer claims; the catalogue does not average them or turn either into a measured weight.

| Model | Product-page figure | Selected technical-table figure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RD-R55S | 193 g, printed p. 7 | 195 g, printed p. 24 |
| RD-R42S | 288 g, printed p. 9 | 265 g, printed p. 24; RD-R42 is separately 288 g |
| FD-R730F | 117 g, printed p. 8 | 119 g for the F mount, printed p. 22 |
| CS-G110, 11–40T | 435 g, printed p. 17 | 434.5 g, printed p. 26 |
| CS-G110, 11–42T | 442 g, printed p. 17 | 441.5 g, printed p. 26 |

**RD-M46S** is explicitly named with 307 g and 35T capacity on printed p. 20. The option row on printed p. 25 instead names **RD-M48S**, with a 39T capacity spanning that part of the table. These identities are not silently merged: RD-M46S uses p. 20, its smallest-sprocket limit remains null, and no RD-M48S record is inferred. RD-M62L's product prose calls it a short cage despite its model suffix; its cage classification is omitted rather than inferred from the letter L. Its 285 g, 42T largest sprocket and 47T capacity come from the exact technical-table column.

Earlier launch dates, additional mount-specific masses and later catalogue generations can be added when the corresponding manufacturer references are reviewed.
