# Shimano 1994 STI early MTB inventory and weight review

Reviewed on **23 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 1994 Shimano STI English/Spanish manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1994_Shimano_STI_ENG_Spanishl.pdf), cross-checked against the original [1994 dealer product manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1994_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf). The STI PDF has **123 pages**, with English/Spanish service sheets interleaved. Citations identify both PDF page and printed section/page; dealer PDF/printed numbers match.

This pass adds **24 components**, enriches **nine existing records** and publishes **six newly qualified weights**. Alivio MC10 expands from three to **17** components, STX MC30 from three to **15**, and Altus CT50 from three to **six**. There are no new groupsets. After this batch the catalogue contains four manufacturers, 272 groupsets and **2,019 components**. Existing IDs, model numbers, primary documents and previously known masses are preserved.

## Six sourced weights

| Model | Weight | Reference and qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RD-MC10 | 261 g | Dealer page 31, exact seven-speed rear model; STI PDF 30 / printed 5-7 identifies its GS reference |
| FD-MC10 | 146 g | Dealer page 31, exact bottom-pull clamp model; its sole documented band is 28.0–28.6 mm, also confirmed in STI PDF 24 / printed 5-1 |
| FC-MC10 | 843 g | Dealer page 31 explicitly selects 170 mm; 42/34/24T is printed in the same model row, with the bottom bracket listed separately |
| ST-MC10 | 415 g per pair | Dealer page 31 explicitly identifies ST-MC10 and a complete pair, 3×7 and 2.5-finger levers |
| BB-LP20 | 341 g | Dealer page 29 explicitly selects the 113 mm LL113 square-taper spindle |
| CS-HG70-C | 299 g | Dealer pages 29/31: seven-speed ac, 11/13/15/18/21/24/28T, including spacers; lockring inclusion follows the contribution convention |

RD-MC10, FD-MC10 and FC-MC10 are existing records whose previously unknown weights are now filled. The other three are new references. The dealer table's cassette note reads **“ac-group & spacers”**; the model and tooth sequence both identify seven sprockets. CS-HG70-C is retained as its exact historical code rather than aliased to later CS-HG70 or HG70-7 records.

The FD-MC10 weight does not require choosing between several bands: the service table gives only **S**, defined as 28.0–28.6 mm, for this exact model. Its neighbouring **FD-MC11** has S/M options and a top-route option; those are not transferred to FD-MC10. Neither FD-MC11 nor the other neighbouring MC11 assemblies inherit MC10 masses.

| Altus CT50 | ST-CT50 3×6 Uni-lever pair, SL-CT50 3×6 thumb-shifter pair, BB-CS11 with the documented 122.5 mm spindle |

The [front-drive sheet, STI PDF 24 / printed 5-1](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1994_Shimano_STI_ENG_Spanishl.pdf#page=24), and [rear-drive sheet, PDF 30 / printed 5-7](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1994_Shimano_STI_ENG_Spanishl.pdf#page=30), explicitly identify the STX and Alivio combinations. MC11 options remain within the existing early Alivio range; the 1994 reference does not backdate those options to the range's introduction year.

The [brake sheet, PDF 36 / printed 5-14](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1994_Shimano_STI_ENG_Spanishl.pdf#page=36), gives exact arm sizes: **BR-MC30 M**, **BR-MC31 L**, **BR-MC10 M**, **BR-MC11 L** and **BR-MC15 S**. STX uses dynamic-type unit links; Alivio uses alignment-type links. These are complete front/rear brake-pair references with unknown weights pending explicit inclusion of pads and mounting bolts. No generic M/L choice is substituted for a model-specific arm size.

All new shifters represent complete left/right controls. The paired front/rear service sheets establish the front and rear configurations; weights are not obtained by doubling a side. ST-MC30 retains its existing 385 g pair weight and gains the 22.2 mm clamp / 2.5-finger reference. The existing BB-UN51 gains explicit STX MC30 membership instead of a duplicate record; its description now covers both STX memberships.

## Derailleur limits and crank references

RD-MC10 and new RD-MC11 gain all four directly printed dealer limits: **35T total capacity, 28T largest sprocket, 11T smallest sprocket and 18T front difference**. The STI sheet confirms GS and seven-speed indexing. RD-MC30 retains its existing limits and long-cage description, with the STI **GS** code recorded separately as a service reference; its 266 g dealer weight is preserved.

The [Altus rear-drive sheet, PDF 45 / printed 6-5](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1994_Shimano_STI_ENG_Spanishl.pdf#page=45), explicitly distinguishes **28T largest sprocket with a triple crank** from **32T with a single chainring**. RD-CT50 selects the triple reference, **34T capacity / 28T largest / 14T smallest**, and records the separate single-chainring 32T option with a visible explanatory note. Its maximum front difference remains unknown; no value is calculated from the accompanying crank.

The [Altus front-drive sheet, PDF 41 / printed 6-1](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1994_Shimano_STI_ENG_Spanishl.pdf#page=41), identifies **FC-CT50 170 mm, 48/38/28T**, triple FD-CT50 and BB-CS11. The crank reference explicitly excludes the separately listed bottom bracket. Compatibility alternatives SL-TY31, FD-TY30 and FC-TY21 are not imported as CT50-specific parts.

New FC-MC30 and FC-MC11 references select **170 mm, 42/34/24T**, excluding bottom brackets; 175 mm remains an available length. Both original tables print **95/58 mm** bolt circles for these models. That figure is transcribed without silently changing 95 to the more familiar 94 mm or copying later MC32 specifications.

## Remaining limits

Hub figures in the dealer tables do not establish skewer exclusion, so all four new hub masses remain unknown. Model-specific ST-MC11, FC-MC11, RD-MC11, BB-LP30, CS-HG50-C and CS-HG60-C weights are not filled from adjacent models. The existing Chromica-suffixed MC31 Special Edition identities are preserved; this pass does not assert that unsuffixed STI names replace those exact finish/route references.

This is a targeted early-MTB review, not a complete audit of all 123 pages. XT/LX, RX100, headset/pedal and additional-product sheets remain further review material. The Spanish sheets can corroborate English specifications but are not counted as additional components.
