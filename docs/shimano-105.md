# Shimano 105 generations

The [component weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md) set the contribution standard. Canonical weights have been audited against those rules. Noncompliant figures are withheld as explicit unknowns; the source notes below distinguish the selected replacement references from older publications.

The catalogue covers all nine Shimano 105 road-series generations, from Golden Arrow to R7100. R7100 has separate mechanical and Di2 groupset records because Shimano offers two shifting systems with different levers and derailleurs. That gives ten browsable groupsets, with 102 component identities and 107 memberships. This is generation coverage with sourced component variants; it does not claim every finish, revision, crank length or sprocket combination.

[Shimano's 105 history](https://www.shimano.com/en/100th/history/products/result.html?id=30) supplies the first eight introduction years. Its chronology is loaded from the manufacturer's [product-history data](https://www.shimano.com/en/100th/history/products/products.csv). These are introduction years, which can precede a catalogue's model-year label: 105 SC is listed under 1989, for example, and appears in the dealer manual published in August 1989 for 1990.

The [Shimano 105 Di2 introduction](https://bike.shimano.com/stories/article/shimano-105-di2-it-is-a-new-day.html) marks the original group's fortieth anniversary in 2022. [Bridgestone's September 2023 announcement](https://www.bscycle.co.jp/anchor/news/2023/09/2024.html) documents bikes using the new mechanical R7100 system. Shimano's [2025–2026 line-up, PDF pages 65–66](https://productinfo.shimano.com/pdfs/product/archive/2025-2026_Line-up_chart_v033_en.pdf#page=65), distinguishes the two systems and identifies their common parts.

Only documented start years are recorded; end years are not collected. The 1051 and 1056 updates, 5501/5504 crank revisions, triple/compact variants and RD-5701 revision stay within their parent families. They do not become extra generations solely because an individual model number changed.

## Weight and compatibility notes

- Every known weight identifies its configuration. The 1050 and 105 SC cranks exclude separately listed bottom brackets, as do the 5500 crank weights. The older 5600/5700 publications include the bottom bracket in their crank totals; those values are now withheld. Later Shimano handbook references supply crank-only weights for FC-5750 (**716 g**, 170 mm, 50/34T), FC-5703 (**831 g**, 170 mm, 50/39/30T) and FC-5800 (**757 g**, 170 mm, 50/34T). Their records cite the exact pages. R7000/R7100 reference figures already exclude the bottom bracket.
- Missing mandatory weight configurations cause the weight to be withheld. Optional source details such as a hub spoke count remain explicitly unknown. Available options do not establish which one supplied the reference weight. Later handbooks can list different figures; the catalogue preserves the cited edition rather than averaging them.
- Shifters are listed as complete left/right pairs, with a pair weight or an explicit unknown. ST-R7000 now has one complete left/right pair record. Its former individual-side IDs are reserved as aliases, and their URLs redirect to the pair. The weight remains null because the selected reference supplies only individual side weights. The R7100 lever figures are pairs. Platform pedal descriptions state toe-clip inclusions, and clipless pedal figures exclude separately listed cleats.
- **Hub weights exclude skewers.** The 2008 publication separates HB-5600's 150 g hub from its 60 g skewer and FH-5600's 353 g hub from its 64 g skewer. Older FH-1050-6, FH-1055 and FH-5500 entries retain unknown weights where the selected publication does not establish hub-only mass. No skewer deduction is guessed. HB-1055-F is now listed with an explicit unknown weight: the 1991 catalogue’s 216 g includes quick release. Other older front hubs remain coverage gaps.
- Every rear derailleur has the four required limit fields. Golden Arrow's smallest-sprocket values remain null. Its short-cage independent front difference also remains null: the 1984 combined-capacity table does not establish an unconditional maximum. [February 1985 service instructions SI-N-83](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/N-83/SI-N-83-000-00-ENG.pdf) give the GS model a 22T front difference and show its largest sprocket changing with the fork end: 34T requires the specified 30 mm bracket/end. Those conditions appear in the record.
- RD-5701-GS supports a largest sprocket of 32T with a double chainset or 30T with a triple, and front differences of 16T or 22T respectively. Its record states these conditions. Individual maxima and total capacity must all be respected; the largest published front and rear ranges cannot always be combined.
- The eight-speed 105 SC update is not the same configuration as its seven-speed introduction. Membership means that a component belongs to the series, not that every listed variant can be combined.

R7100 mechanical and Di2 share FC-R7100, CS-R7101-12, BB-RS501 and PD-R7000 identities. PD-R7000 is also listed in R7000. Mechanical RD-R7100/FD-R7100/ST-R7120 and electronic RD-R7150/FD-R7150/ST-R7170 remain separate. The earlier CS-R7100 cassette is not substituted for CS-R7101 or asserted to support the mechanical system. Brake calipers now have a [supported category and initial records](brake-calipers.md). Chains, Di2 batteries and complete wheels are outside the current component taxonomy; missing categories and variants mean **not yet catalogued**, not proof they never existed.

Standalone brake-lever models and their source limitations are documented in the [brake-lever source notes](brake-levers.md). Integrated shift/brake controls remain in the Shifters category.

The [1991 dealer catalogue review](shimano-1991.md) adds HB-1055-F and the shared seven-speed CS-HG70 I-group membership to 105 SC. Its 286 g 105 SC cassette figure conflicts with 325 g for the same model/gearing in the RX100 table; the canonical weight is now null, with both references retained. The BB-1055 reference still does not identify which spindle length was weighed.

## FH-1056 contributor measurement

On 8 November 2026, the project owner reported personally weighing a **36-hole Shimano FH-1056 rear hub at 345 g without its skewer**. This is a contributor measurement of that configuration, not a manufacturer-listed figure or a claim about every FH-1056 variant. Axle dimensions, scale resolution and component condition were not reported.

The catalogue uses this direct measurement instead of the 1993 catalogue’s 427 g quick-release-inclusive listing. No assumed skewer mass was subtracted. The Shimano exploded view remains the technical-document reference for model identity; it is not the source of the 345 g measurement.

## Additional brake calipers

1050 now includes the original BR-1050-49 and BR-1050-57 single-pivot pairs; the December 1987 manufacturer catalogue gives **335 g for the 49 type only**. 105 SC includes the separate BR-1055-49 and BR-1055-57 dual-pivot pairs, with **360 g for the 49 type only** in the 1990 dealer manual. Both 57-type weights remain unknown.

BR-5700 retains an unknown pair weight because the selected 2010–2011 specification table has no weight row. BR-5800 uses the explicit **388 g/pair** reference with standard R55C4 pads from the 2014–2015 specifications. Both include factory mounting hardware in the intended reference configuration. The [caliper source guide](brake-calipers.md#additional-shimano-road-calipers) records the exact publications and remaining gaps, including the later 105 SC BR-1056 revision.

The [2005 handbook review](shimano-2005.md) adds the later BR-5501 caliper pair at **353 g** with R55C pads, separate from BR-5500 at 352 g. It also supplies the explicit **260 g pair** reference for the shared BL-R600 brake levers.

The [dealer-manual review](shimano-dealer-manuals.md) fills the existing BR-5600 caliper pair at **359 g** with factory R55C2 pads and mounting bolts, from the 2008 manual’s F&R row. The selected reference does not identify its mounting-nut/bolt length.

## SM-FC5600 bottom-bracket cups

The [2006 Shimano dealer manual, page 139](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2006_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=139) explicitly identifies **SM-FC5600** as the separately available HOLLOWTECH II BB cup unit for FC-5600/5603 in the 105 range. It now has its own bottom-bracket record in 105 5600. [Page 195](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2006_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=195) gives combined crankset/BB weights but no separate cup-unit weight, so SM-FC5600 remains `weight_g: null`. No weight is derived by subtraction, and the existing crankset weights remain withheld.

The [front-derailleur review](shimano-front-derailleur-weights.md) resolves FD-1056-B at **105 g for the 28.0–28.6 mm band**, using the 1994 dealer manual p. 52. Its earlier unknown weight is no longer needed because the reference now identifies the band range.
