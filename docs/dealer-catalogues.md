# Catalogue scans and specification handbooks

This source review on 10 November 2026 supplements the [1997 Campagnolo review](campagnolo-1997-specifications.md). It adds facts to **42 existing component records**: **16 previously unknown weights**, missing limits in **13 rear derailleurs**, tooth limits in **12 front derailleurs**, and pulley-centre dimensions in three further rear derailleurs. Some records receive both a weight and limits. No identities, groupset memberships or primary technical-document URLs change.

These are original manufacturer publications, including consumer catalogues and technical handbooks as well as the dealer material sought. Archive commentary and retailer product descriptions are not used as specification evidence. High-resolution scans were checked visually; OCR and thumbnail readings alone were not accepted.

The Campagnolo catalogue calls its weights averages for the lightest available specification. The six selected rear rows have no alternative cage/finish options in their respective table rows; the exact generation and existing assembly-code evidence are preserved. These figures do not describe earlier components with the same group name. The pedal publication explicitly says “pair”; cleat and additional hardware inclusions are not invented.

The FC-7700 figure is for **175 mm**, not 170 mm. The [guidelines](weights-guidelines.md) prefer a 170 mm reference when available but do not permit converting or relabelling a documented 175 mm weight. The 2001 crank table identifies **FC-7701**, so its figure is not substituted for FC-7700.

## Front limits and cage dimensions

Campagnolo's 2015 tables support these front-derailleur limits without supplying an unambiguous mount-specific weight:

- FD15-SR2B / FD15-SR2BS2: 16T capacity, 56T largest and 34T smallest chainring.
- FD15-RE2B / FD15-RE2BS2: 16T capacity, 55T largest and 34T smallest chainring.
- FD15-CH2B / FD15-CH2BS2: 16T capacity, 56T largest and 34T smallest chainring.
- FD15-SR2BEPS / FD15-RE2BEPS / FD15-CH2BEPS: 16T capacity, 55T largest and 34T smallest chainring.
- FD11-VLB2B / FD11-VLB2C2 / FD11-VLB2C5: 16T capacity, 55T largest and 34T smallest chainring.

Veloce RD11-VLBXS and RD11-VLBXM have documented 55 mm and 72.5 mm pulley-centre distances respectively; Athena Triple RD13-ATB13 has 82 mm. These are physical cage dimensions, not a derived capacity or an invented SS/GS code. Their listed weights cover black/silver finish options without identifying which is the lightest reference, so weights remain null.

## Conflicts and exclusions

- **Existing known weights are preserved.** In particular, the owner's RD-4400-SS 264 g, RD-3300-SS 266 g and RD-A551-SS 273 g measurements remain unchanged. The 2001 catalogue's 265 g RD-4400-SS claim does not overwrite the measured 264 g.
- **RD-7700 capacity:** the 2001 catalogue says 29T, while the existing cited SI-5TJ1G service instructions say 26T. The service-instruction value remains; the new weight is explicitly identified as the 2001 catalogue reference, and the record displays a source-conflict note. Other existing sourced limits are not overwritten by a conflicting catalogue row.
- **Campagnolo front weights:** the tables combine braze-on units and optional clamp kits, with an average/lightest-configuration footnote but no explicit mount-to-weight mapping. Front limits can be used; weights remain null.
- **Campagnolo Athena triple front capacity:** the table's 16T figure conflicts with its listed 52T/30T end chainrings. This review does not repair that apparent printing error by calculating a replacement; the row is deferred.
- **Hub weights:** catalogues list weights without establishing skewer exclusion for the individual hubs. The wheel-system note about complete wheels is not applied to standalone hubs. No skewer deduction is estimated.
- **Combined front/rear brake weights, unspecified square-taper spindle weights and control weights without pair evidence** remain excluded under the weight guidelines.
- **RD-3300-A is not RD-3300**, and **FC-7701 is not FC-7700**. Similar names, replacement compatibility or a nearby table column do not establish identity.
