# microSHIFT 2025: ADVENT X, XLE and Marvo LT additions

Reviewed on 22 November 2026 from the original **microSHIFT 2025 Drivetrain** catalogue preserved in the [archived manufacturer publication](https://www.bikecatalogs.com/download/5815). This is the same publication used in the [Acolyte review](mtb-catalogue-followup-2026-11-22.md). The exact tables on PDF spreads **6, 11 and 12** were checked visually against their text, model numbers, gearing and weight columns. Printed page numbers below identify each half of the spreads.

The batch adds **28 components and 26 manufacturer-listed weights**, plus the explicitly named **XLE 11** range. The catalogue now contains **1,702 components across 263 groupsets**. Existing component IDs, weights and document links are unchanged.

## Range identity and shared parts

The manufacturer explicitly heads printed page 17 **XLE 11, 1/2×11-speed**, separately from the existing historical XCD 11 offering. XLE 11 receives a new identity with **2025 as documented catalogue availability**, not an asserted first-introduction date. No XCD record is renamed or moved. Earlier XLE 11 chronology remains a research gap.

The same RD-M665M, RD-M66M, RD-M665L and RD-M66L models are listed in both XLE 11 and XLE 10 tables. Each has **one shared component identity** with both memberships. Compatibility with Shimano Mountain or Sword alone is not used to add memberships in unrelated ranges.

## Conflicting medium-cage figures retained as unknown

| Shared model | XLE 10, printed p. 18 | XLE 11, printed p. 17 | Canonical weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RD-M665M | 373 g | 379 g | null |
| RD-M66M | 301 g | 309 g | null |

Both tables describe these as **10/11-speed medium-cage models** without distinguishing the weighed hardware. The range heading alone does not resolve the discrepancy. The identities and common specifications are added, while weights remain unknown; no value is selected, averaged or reconciled with a deduction.

The medium-cage models have 41T total capacity; the long-cage models have 47T. Both have a **40–46T largest-sprocket compatibility range**, retained alongside the 46T upper value. Smallest sprocket and maximum front difference stay null. ADVENT X’s steel-cage derailleur has 37T total capacity and a 48T largest sprocket; its other two limits remain null.

All new records cite the original manufacturer tables and use explicit null technical-document links under the approved optional-link policy. No source scans or PDFs are redistributed in Git. This review changes catalogue data and source notes, not delivery, caching or component schemas.
