# Shimano RSX and Light Action

## RSX A410/A416

The RSX family has a documented start year of **1994**. Shimano’s *Bicycle System Components ’95* catalogue was published in July 1994: [page 51 introduces RSX](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/shimano_bicycle_system_components_-_95_page_51.html), and [page 53 lists its components and specifications](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/shimano_bicycle_system_components_-_95_page_53.html). The cover’s model year is not substituted for the publication year.

One family record covers original seven-speed A410 equipment and the later eight-speed A416 revision. It includes 23 component identities: four rear-derailleur variants, four double/triple cranks, three front derailleurs, four shifter models, two bottom brackets, four hubs and two cassette configurations. Every component links to Shimano-hosted service instructions or an exploded view.

The seven-speed double FC-A410 uses 46/36T chainrings; FC-A413 uses 46/36/26T. [SI-1DA0A](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/1DA0A/SI-1DA0A-000-00-ENG.pdf) lists BB-LP25 with 110 mm and 118 mm spindles for the respective configurations. The catalogue lists SS and GS rear derailleurs at 28T and 36T total capacity, both with 28T largest and 11T smallest sprockets; maximum front differences are 13T and 20T. Later SI-5UH0B describes an SGS version at 37T capacity. That later figure is not substituted for the catalogue’s GS version.

[SI-5US0B, dated February 1999](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/5US0B/SI-5US0B-000-00-ENG.pdf), explicitly identifies the eight-speed RSX system: ST-A416/ST-A417, FD-A416/FD-A417, FC-A416/FC-A417, BB-LP27, RD-A416/RD-A416-GS, FH-A416 and CS-HG50-8. SS limits are 28T total capacity, 28T largest sprocket, 11T smallest and 14T front difference; GS limits are 36T, 30T, 11T and 22T respectively. [SI-1C20A](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/1C20A/SI-1C20A-000-00-ENG.pdf) documents the 52/42T double and 52/42/30T triple cranks and their bottom-bracket configurations. These later parts are not dated to the family’s 1994 start.

The original seven-speed CS-HG50 reference is 11–24T. It has a distinct record from the existing 13–23T I-group cassette used by Exage; a shared base model name does not erase a different tooth configuration.

The RSX columns in the July 1994 specification table leave weights blank. All RSX weights remain null until a compliant sourced configuration is recorded. Neighbouring RX100 weights are not copied into the RSX columns, and hub weights must exclude skewers. Brakes, standalone brake levers and chains remain outside the current taxonomy. Later mount and finish variants are not claimed to be exhaustively catalogued.

## Light Action L series

Light Action is represented as a rear-derailleur family, rather than an invented complete matched groupset. Its start year is **1984**, supported by the September 1984 footer on Shimano’s [RD-L513 exploded view](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/RD-L513-0692/EV-RD-L513-0692A.pdf) and the October 1984 [SI-N-78A instructions](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/N-78A/SI-N-78A-000-00-ENG.pdf). “L series” is the catalogue family label for these related Light Action models.

The ten component records include original RD-L512-SGS, RD-L513-SS/GS and RD-L514-SGS, plus all three documented cages of RD-L532 and RD-L541. The original instructions provide capacity and front-difference limits and warn that the frame and fork end affect usable capacity. They do not establish SIS compatibility for those original models.

The later references are [Shimano’s December 1987 catalogue, scan 31](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/shimano_bicycle_system_components_december_1987_-_scan_31.html) and [SI-P-51B](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/P-51B/SI-P-51B-000-00-ENG.pdf):

| Model | Weight | Total capacity | Largest sprocket | Front difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RD-L532-SS | 272 g | 28T | 28T | 13T |
| RD-L532-GS | 293 g | 34T | 34T | 14T |
| RD-L532-SGS | 306 g | 40T | 34T | 20T |
| RD-L541-SS | 314 g | 28T | 28T | 13T |
| RD-L541-GS | 342 g | 36T | 30T | 20T |
| RD-L541-SGS | 345 g | 38T | 32T | 20T |

These are manufacturer-listed derailleur-only weights for the named cage, not estimates. The L532 catalogue specifies SIS compatibility only for SS; GS/SGS must not be treated as indexed-compatible merely because the series name matches. All three L541 cages are listed as SIS compatible. Cage codes come from the documents, not from guessed capacities. The smallest sprocket remains null because the cited tables do not specify it.

Further Light Action revisions and other component types require their own source evidence. A service instruction’s compatibility recommendation alone does not prove membership of an independently sold crank, hub or headset in this family.
