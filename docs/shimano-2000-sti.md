# Shimano 2000 STI Tiagra and Sora triple review

Reviewed on **23 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 2000 Shimano STI manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/2000_Shimano_STI.pdf). Its archive disclaimer identifies the information as updated in **2000**. The PDF contains **261 pages**, including later technical-support sheets and rotated service pages. In the selected road sections below, PDF and printed page numbers match; that is not assumed for the entire file.

This targeted pass adds **five components** and enriches **six existing component identities**, with source citations on the two existing groupsets. It adds no new ranges or weights. The catalogue contains four manufacturers, 271 groupsets and 1,959 components after the batch. Existing IDs, known masses and primary technical-document URLs are preserved; the two shared bottom brackets receive neutral descriptive names as their road memberships are added.

Both new front-derailleur sheets explicitly list **band and braze-on options**, with **28.6 and 31.8 mm** band sizes. Those are available mount configurations, not an actual weighed clamp reference. Their weights stay null; neither a mount nor a band size is arbitrarily selected to justify a mass.

FC-4403 selects the documented **170 mm, 52/42/30T** reference, excluding the separately listed bottom bracket. FC-3303's exact rings and bolt circles are documented, but the selected chainring sheet does not establish its arm length. Its arm length stays unresolved, and no FC-4403 length or weight is transferred.

## Sora control pair

The [Sora STI service table on page 93](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/2000_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=93) separates left and right models. The double uses ST-3300 on both sides; the triple uses **ST-3303 left and ST-3300 right**. The new composite model label describes that exact left/right assembly reference, not a newly invented manufacturer SKU.

Its weight remains null. The existing 445 g ST-3300 double-pair weight is preserved and is not transferred to the triple, doubled or split into guessed side weights. The source does not publish separate side masses to add together.

The [Tiagra STI table on page 78](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/2000_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=78), by contrast, lists **ST-4400** for both double and triple configurations. The existing record gains explicit available front speeds of two and three. No ST-4403 control identity is invented, and its existing published pair weight stays unchanged.

## Shared bottom brackets

BB-UN52 remains one canonical identity. It gains Tiagra membership from the [FC-4400/FC-4403 table on page 88](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/2000_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=88), which explicitly lists **110 mm for the double and 113 mm for the triple**, and Sora membership from the named STI drivetrain chart on page 93.

The [detailed FD-3303 sheet on page 98](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/2000_Shimano_STI.pdf#page=98) separately lists **BB-UN40, 113 mm**. That existing record gains Sora membership with its own citation. The general Sora chart and detailed sheet therefore identify different bottom brackets. Both printed references are retained; neither is rewritten as the other or claimed to prove universal interchangeability. No replacement mass is guessed.

The existing BB-UN52 spindle-option list comes from its older MTB table and is preserved. The 2000 road table's printed 110 mm reference is stated in the new citation; it is not silently normalised to the older 110.5 mm entry. The records' generic names now say “Square-taper bottom bracket” to avoid presenting a shared model as exclusively MTB-branded. Their IDs, slugs, masses and primary documents do not change.
