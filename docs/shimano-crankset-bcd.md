# Shimano crankset BCD source review

Reviewed on **27 November 2026**. This pass adds explicit bolt circle diameters to **63 existing cranksets**, using each model’s original Shimano exploded view. The links below identify the exact drawing and PDF page. Scanned statements were visually checked after OCR; native-text statements were checked against the rendered drawing.

Existing IDs, model numbers, groupset memberships, weights, weight descriptions and primary technical documents are unchanged. Unknown weights remain unknown. These are dimensional facts, not a claim of chainring interchangeability: bolt arrangement, ring shape and other manufacturer compatibility requirements still matter.

## Sourced diameters

Multiple diameters are listed from the outer mounting circle inward. For triples with one outer/middle mounting circle, both rings use the first diameter.

## Model-specific distinctions

- **FC-M960:** the drawing explicitly gives **146 mm outer, 102 mm middle and 64 mm inner**. This is not replaced by the later FC-M970’s 104 / 64 mm pattern.
- **FC-7803:** the drawing explicitly gives **130 / 92 mm**. The inner diameter is not copied from FC-7703 or another triple.
- **FC-MC31-CH:** the original drawing prints **95 / 58 mm**. That source value is preserved rather than normalized to a more familiar nominal size.
- **FC-RX820-1 / FC-RX820-2:** their shared drawing says **110 mm for outer and single chainrings; 80 mm for the inner chainring**. The single-ring -1 record therefore gets 110 mm only; the double-ring -2 gets 110 / 80 mm.
- **FC-A452:** its own drawing explicitly prints 130 mm; no inner-ring diameter is invented.
- **FC-A413:** its own RSX triple drawing explicitly prints 110 mm for the outer/middle rings and 74 mm for the inner ring. The parts list’s pack weights are not used as complete-crank weights.

## Remaining gaps

Drawings reviewed for FC-6200, FC-6210, FC-6300, FC-7000, FC-7200, FC-7300, FC-7400, FC-7500, FC-6207, FC-M950, FC-M739-4, FC-MC10, FC-M330 and FC-M290 did not yield an explicit BCD statement in this pass. Their unknown fields remain unchanged; no standard diameter is inferred from appearance or compatibility. Reviewed early Adamas/Altus drawings likewise remain unchanged where no explicit diameter was found.

The reviewed 2025 microSHIFT Sword/Sword Black catalogue pages specify configurations and spindle details but do not provide a BCD statement on those pages. No microSHIFT diameter is added on that evidence.
