# Shimano 1986: complete catalogue gap review

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 from the [owner-supplied original Shimano catalogue](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xB-btuu8aJkkBD1JzsDKX5ki0wtZ1U9_/view). The scan contains **20 PDF pages**. Its back cover states copyright **August 1986**, printing code `0786`, and “Printed in West Germany XBC”. The edition is identified as 1986 without inferring a different date from PDF metadata or treating the print code as an exact publication date. References below use **PDF pages**.

The review adds **43 component records**, **one named MTB control series** and **24 previously unknown weights**: 21 new-record figures and three existing-record gaps. The catalogue contains 262 groupsets and 1,604 components after this batch. Existing IDs, slugs, model numbers and primary technical-document URLs are preserved. All new primary documents are Shimano-hosted assembly drawings; the Drive scan supplies supporting manufacturer specifications and membership evidence. No scan bytes or images are committed.

The existing **600 EX 6207** family gains explicit 1986 evidence that its 6208 SIS revision is listed alongside the 6207 components. Its stable identity and 1983 start are unchanged. The separately headed **AT 50 series** is added as an MTB control range because it is explicitly presented in the off-road section; its 1986 start records availability in this edition, not an asserted first launch. It contains BR-AT50, BL-AT50 and SL-AT50; a new group is not created solely from a compatibility chart or model-prefix similarity.

RD-M700, FD-M700 and PD-6207 are the three existing records whose previously unknown weights are filled. Other figures are new-record weights. The clamp conversion is exact unit conversion, not a new measurement. The caliper pair figures are not divided; their exact factory drawings identify standard pads and mounting bolts. Optional fixing-bolt variants are not asserted as the weighed hardware.

### Pair references and hub exclusions

**PD-6207’s 440 g is a directly published pair figure**, not a calculation from the earlier 220 g single/unspecified reference. This resolves the limitation documented in the [1984 review](shimano-600-ex-1984.md).

HB-MN72’s nut-fastened front and rear assemblies are explicitly distinguished from HB-MN72-QR in both the catalogue and manufacturer drawings. They have hub nuts and no QR skewers. No nut or axle mass is subtracted. The 260/348 g QR figures remain unsuitable for canonical weights, as do the QR-unspecified FH-6208/HB-6207/105 figures. The records for QR variants express the no-skewer requirement for a future measurement; they do not pretend the physical assemblies have no quick release.

## Inventory and model-reference decisions

### 600 and 105 controls

The new 600 SIS pairs are **SL-6208-6FCAI, -6FCBI, -6BCAI and -6FAI**, using the full six-speed model codes printed on Shimano’s drawing. The 1986 catalogue abbreviates these to SL-6208-FCAI/FCBI/BCAI/FAI; those catalogue labels are retained separately. M5/M4.5 and band/braze-on variants are not merged into one invented weighted assembly. The 69 g FCAI figure does not explicitly identify a pair basis, so all four remain unweighed.

New 105 pairs are **SL-1050-BCAI, -BCBI, -FCBI and -FAI**. The existing SL-1050-FCAI record and its later explicit weight reference are preserved. The 68 g FCAI figure is not transferred to other mounts. **SL-6207-FC** is separately added for the braze-on C friction pair, without borrowing SL-6207-BA’s mass.

**FD-6207-F and FD-1050-F** add the explicit braze-on assemblies. FD-1050’s 98 g band reference is not copied to its braze-on variant. FD-6207’s shared 110 g reference does not identify the weighed mount, so its unknown weights remain unchanged. **HB-1050-F, HB-6207-F and FH-6208-6** add exact hub identities with null weights; the catalogue’s FH-6208-R six-speed label is reconciled to the full FH-6208-6 code on the manufacturer silver-gear drawing.

### Round and Biopace cranks

The ordinary **FC-6207** record retains its 672 g, 170 mm, 52/42T round-ring reference. The separate Biopace record keeps manufacturer model number **FC-6207** with a distinct stable ID/slug and descriptive name; no fictitious manufacturer `-BP` suffix is added. Its exact profile and 667 g weight come from page 16. The Shimano drawing identifies the base FC-6207 assembly and round-ring service parts; it is not misrepresented as an illustration of the Biopace reference. The original manufacturer catalogue supplies that variant evidence.

**FC-6206** is explicitly the 700 g Biopace triple and has its own manufacturer drawing identifying the Biopace assembly. Neither crankset weight includes the separately listed bottom bracket. The 326/330 g BB-6207 figures offer different spindle/shell configurations without selecting the weighed spindle; BB-6207 remains unweighed, while its documented spindle options are expanded to 116/119 mm for doubles and 121.5/124.5 mm for triples. BB-1050 likewise does not acquire the unspecific 327 g figure.

The catalogue’s FC-1050 664 g, 170 mm, 52/42T Biopace reference corroborates the existing **FC-1050-BP** record. It is not duplicated as another component merely because the earlier edition abbreviates the model label.

### XT and AT 50

The XT inventory adds **SL-M700, BL-M700, BR-MC70, RD-M700-SP, FD-M700-GS, PD-GX10, PD-MX15** and separate nut/QR HB-MN72 front/rear assemblies. PD-MX15 is explicitly shown in the XT section: its MX model prefix is not used to exclude a documented MTB membership. The independently labelled AT 50 controls are recorded under that range rather than inferred as XT members solely from sharing a page.

BR-MC70 remains a complete front/rear set with an unknown mass: the unpositioned 260 g source figure explicitly excludes mounting shafts. It does not meet the complete pads/bolts rule. PD-GX10’s 526 g reflector figure lacks an explicit pair basis and is not doubled.

### Light Action

The new **L523 SS/GS/SGS and L525 GS/SGS** records use exact cage-specific manufacturer drawings. **L523 is not transposed into L532** despite similar model numbers and overlapping weights. Existing L532 records and their later references remain unchanged. L422’s three explicitly listed mounts (**BA, FA and S**) are added as complete pairs with unknown weights; the 95/145 g mount figures do not explicitly establish pair totals.

## Derailleur conditions

- RD-6208 has 28T total capacity, 28T largest and 12T smallest sprocket; the maximum front difference remains unknown.
- Standard RD-M700 has 40T total, 34T largest with PR/SFR fork ends and 22T front difference. Its smallest sprocket remains unknown.
- RD-M700-SP selects the 42T total reference with FE-SF21/SF22; the table also gives 42T for FE-SF20/EF20 and 40T for FE-UF20. Its 34T largest sprocket applies with Shimano fork ends. A separate front-difference figure is not inferred from the normal M700 assembly.
- L523 short-cage references use any Shimano fork ends; its long/super-long entries specify FE-SF21/SF22/PR22. L525 specifies FE-SF21/SF22/PR20. These conditions are retained alongside the canonical capacities.
- [Shimano SI-P-4](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/P-4/SI-P-4-000-00-ENG.pdf), page 1, explicitly supplies L523/L525 front differences: SS 13T, GS 14T, SGS 20T. It qualifies capacities with the B-tension screw fully tightened and notes dependence on fork/frame dimensions. Smallest-sprocket fields remain unknown.

## Remaining leads

- **Z components, page 18:** the catalogue and manufacturer documents identify Z501/Z503/Z505 cages, Z202/Z204/Z206 fronts, Z401/Z408 controls, Z570/Z640/Z720/Z790 calipers and Z32x levers. The page is headed general “Shimano Components”, not an identified groupset. These are valuable future references, but they are not assigned invented memberships. SI-N-86 confirms exact rear models and capacity fields; it does not establish a whole-group identity or riding category by itself.
- The Z brake table provides separate front/rear weights by reach. These should be retained as position-specific references when a supported range/membership is established, not divided or combined into undocumented variants. The Z front-derailleur figures also need actual clamp configuration evidence.
- **FH/HB-7400-ATB:** the XT section lists these variants, but exact current manufacturer-hosted ATB documents were not found. Road 7400 drawings are not used to assume the ATB assemblies are identical.
- **PD-T100:** reflector/no-reflector figures are useful leads, but neither an explicit pair basis nor a named catalogue group is established here.
- Threaded MF freewheels, track sprockets, chains and derailleur guards are outside the current component-type set. They are not represented as cassettes or complete derailleurs to fit the schema.
- Missing model-specific or mounting configurations remain null rather than inheriting a nearby variant’s weight. This review covers the entire edition without claiming every general component can yet be imported.
