# Contributor-reported weights

These notes preserve weight reports and distinguish them from manufacturer specifications. Follow the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md) when supplying further details.

## Rear derailleur weights reported on 9 November 2026

The project owner reported these Shimano rear-derailleur weights:

| Model | Reported weight | Configuration and catalogue status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RD-4400-SS | 264 g | Tiagra short-cage rear derailleur; recorded |
| RD-3300-SS | 266 g | Sora short-cage rear derailleur; recorded |
| RD-A551-SS | 273 g | RX100 eight-speed short-cage rear derailleur; recorded |

The project owner confirmed personally weighing all three derailleurs and identified the RD-A551 as the SS short-cage version. These are contributor measurements, not manufacturer-listed figures. Scale resolution, component condition and included hardware were not reported.

Shimano’s [RD-A551 exploded drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/RD-A551-1390/EV-RD-A551-1390A.pdf) identifies distinct SS short-cage and GS long-cage versions. The confirmed 273 g measurement belongs only to RD-A551-SS; it is not assigned to the GS version. The drawing establishes model identity and RX100 membership, not the measured weight.
