# MTB catalogue weights — 22 November 2026

This follow-up adds **ten components, all with manufacturer-listed weights**: nine microSHIFT Acolyte variants and the missing Shimano CS-MT62 cassette. The catalogue now contains **1,674 components across 262 groupsets**. Existing component IDs, technical links, weights and unknowns are preserved.

## Manufacturer publications reviewed

- Original **microSHIFT 2025 Drivetrain** catalogue, [archived publication](https://www.bikecatalogs.com/download/5815#page=8), PDF spread 8 / printed pp. 11–12. This is the same original publication checked in the [earlier source review](catalogue-review-2026-11-21.md#microshift-2025-catalogue). Its [official download](https://www.microshift.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Microshift-2025-Catalog-20240911.pdf) was previously found in the manufacturer’s catalogue index; the exact entries were read from the archived original, not archive commentary.
- Original **Shimano 1989 catalogue**, [owner-supplied scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DjMjm22K9deQRXb2jHrivPKqs0Y_FAYr/view), PDF p. 27, in the Deore II spread. It explicitly identifies the cassette’s seven sprockets and the 13–30T weighed reference. See the [edition review](shimano-mtb-1989.md).

The tables were checked visually against their model numbers and extracted text. The microSHIFT e-bike controls use the manufacturer’s **-ER** suffix.

Cassette lockring inclusion is assumed under the existing weight guidelines; no lockring mass is estimated or added. All five new shifters are genuine single right-hand controls for Acolyte’s 1×8 system, not left/right pairs. Their weight basis and speed fields preserve that distinction.

## Revisions, gearing and limits

The Ver.2 derailleurs are separately published models. Their figures do **not** fill the original **RD-M5185M SpringLock** weight, which stays null. Both new revisions have 41T total capacity and a 40–46T largest-sprocket compatibility range. The upper value is recorded as 46T with the full range retained. Smallest sprocket and maximum front difference remain null; neither is inferred from the offered cassettes or total capacity. Compatibility is explicitly **Acolyte only**.

The two new CS-H083 references do not replace the existing **12–46T / 576 g** cassette or its 12/15/18/21/24/30/37/46T sprockets. A shared model number is not a shared weighed configuration. The existing SL-M6185-R / 122 g control likewise retains its identity and source.

The Acolyte group’s existing 2021 documented-availability year is unchanged. Inclusion of Ver.2 parts in the 2025 edition does not claim they existed at the group’s first recorded availability.

CS-MT62 is now imported under the optional technical-link policy. The old document-link blocker is resolved with explicit `technical_document: null`; the FH-MT62 service instruction is not substituted as a cassette document. All nine new microSHIFT records also use null technical links with actual manufacturer-publication citations. No manufacturer URLs are invented from model-number patterns.

The 1989 BL-MT60/PD-MT60 weight conflicts and QR hub exclusions documented in the prior review remain unresolved and unchanged. Cantilever “pair” figures are not automatically treated as complete front/rear brake sets.
