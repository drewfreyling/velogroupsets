# Shimano 1994: cartridge headsets and missing pedal records

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 from the [manufacturer-hosted 1994 Bicycle System Component Dealer Product Manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1994_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf). The PDF and printed page numbers match. The source tables and model diagrams were checked visually; OCR was used only to locate entries.

This follow-up adds **12 component records**, including **nine manufacturer-listed weights**. The catalogue contains 1,477 component records after this batch. Existing identities, records and weights are unchanged. Each new record cites the exact manufacturer-hosted manual page as its primary technical document.

**HP-6500 belongs to the 600 Ultegra 6400 offering on this page.** Its model number does not establish membership of the later Ultegra 6500 groupset. HP-6400 is listed separately at 100 g and already exists in the catalogue.

The one-inch tables offer multiple thread/race standards without identifying which was weighed. The weight descriptions retain that limitation rather than choosing an English, JIS or French reference. The larger models have explicit BC 1 1/8 inch × 26 TPI or BC 1 1/4 inch × 26 TPI threads and model-specific race dimensions.

The LX table assigns **147 g specifically to HP-M563**, not HP-M564. The STX Special Edition headset row supplies dimensions but no weight. Unknowns remain null; nearby weights are not transferred between steerer sizes or finishes.

## Pedals

All four figures explicitly identify left/right pairs.

| PD-6402 | 600 Ultegra 6400 | 450 g | Clipless pedal pair; the separate 92 g cleat/hardware reference is excluded | 49 |
| PD-M323 | Deore LX M560; STX Special Edition MC31 | 520 g | SPD-compatible pair, each with one SPD side and one conventional shoe side; cleat inclusion unspecified | 21; membership diagram on 27 |
| PD-M350 | STX MC30 | 445 g | MTB platform pair, two-screw toe-clip mounts; accessory inclusion unspecified | 29 |

The **PD-M350** model number on page 29 was checked on the scan: it is not PD-M550. The existing PD-M550 record is not overwritten or treated as a conflicting figure.

## Cross-checks and remaining limits

- Page 53 corroborates existing PD-1055 (433 g pair with toe clips), PD-1056 (460 g pair, cleats/hardware separate) and HP-1055 (101 g) records. They are not duplicated or overwritten. The 1994 headset dimensions are not silently substituted for an earlier reference configuration.
- An illustrated PD-M525 assembly on page 21 does not acquire PD-M323’s 520 g weight. They are distinct models.
- The earlier [1994 MTB/road review](shimano-mtb.md#component-tables) already added weights and components from other tables. This follow-up covers the headsets and pedal gaps above, rather than claiming that the entire manual is exhausted.
- No hub weights, inferred group memberships, accessory deductions or doubled single-pedal figures are added.
