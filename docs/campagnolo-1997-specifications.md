# Campagnolo 1997 weights and capacities

Reviewed on 10 November 2026 following the supplied [1997 sales catalogue scan at Retrobike](https://www.retrobike.co.uk/gallery2/d/118838-2/1997+Campagnolo+Catalog.pdf). Retrobike's Cloudflare challenge blocked retrieval in the research environment. The [Prestacycle/CampyBike archive](https://mail.prestacycle.co.uk/campybike/) provides another copy of the sales catalogue and two technical-specification booklets in its [1994–1999 download](https://mail.prestacycle.co.uk/campybike/downloads/Campagnolo_1994_1999s.zip).

The figures added here come from **1997CampagnoloTechnicalSpecificationsV2.pdf** in that archive. Its cover says *1997 Technical Specifications*; “V2” is the archive filename, not a claimed manufacturer revision number. This copy includes exact assembly codes, weights and configuration tables. Printed pages are two less than PDF page numbers. The file's SHA-256 is `7827802d04ac14b538e865b634f263a86a3d11f13850d3f9e341df5f1d8a8e2b`.

The archive download is large (about 257 MB); extract the named PDF rather than treating its similarly named companion as interchangeable. Component citations identify the filename and both page numbers. Existing approved Disraeli Gears spare-parts drawings remain the technical documents used to identify the assemblies. This review adds supporting citations, without extending the technical-document URL exception or creating new component identities.

The model-specific rear tables label their final column “max. chainr.”, despite showing 26/28-tooth rear gearing. The companion booklet's explicitly labelled “max cog” table and the sales catalogue's “max sprocket” row corroborate these as **largest rear sprockets**, not front-chainring limits.

The documents disagree on total capacity: the companion **1997CampagnoloTechnicalSpecifications.pdf** gives 26T for the standard double models, whereas the code-specific table gives 27T for Record/Chorus/Athena and 28T for Veloce/Mirage/Avanti. The sales catalogue's final table agrees with 27T for the nine-speed Record/Chorus columns, but lists 26T for Athena/Veloce/Mirage/Avanti. The catalogue records use the explicitly identified assembly rows above, with their exact source citation. This is a documented source choice, not a claim that the publications agree or that one archive filename establishes chronological revision order.

Neither table establishes a smallest sprocket or an independent rear-derailleur front-chainring difference. Those fields remain null. A front derailleur's stated capacity is not copied into a rear-derailleur limit, and no missing limit is calculated from cassette options. “Short cage” and “long cage” describe the source variants; no Shimano-style cage code is invented for these Campagnolo assemblies.

## Headsets and front derailleurs

Printed page 1 (PDF page 3) gives the threaded headset weights: **HS-21RE 106 g**, **HS-11CH 107 g**, **HS-11AT 142 g**, **HS-21VL 141 g** and **HSI01MI 142 g**. All use the documented BC 1 inch × 24 tpi thread, 26.4 mm crown-race seat and 30.2 mm cup diameter. Record/Chorus have 36.5 mm total stack; the other three have 41.5 mm.

| FD-31SRE | Braze-on, double | 89 g | 15T | 54T | 13 |

These are braze-on-only weights without a clamp adapter. Clamp variants have separate rows and weights; their figures are not applied to these records.

## Figures left out

- Crankset rows give weights and bottom-bracket spindle dimensions but do not identify the weighed crank-arm length and chainring tooth counts. Those weights remain null under the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md).
- Hub rows give weights without explicitly establishing skewer exclusion. No QR deduction or assumption is made; hub weights remain null.
- Seatpost tables list several available diameters without identifying which diameter the weight describes. No reference diameter is invented.
- The control drawings do not provide a supported left/right pair weight, and the sprocket tables do not supply cassette weights.
- The braze-on Avanti row says **FDI11SAV**, while the existing parts drawing identifies **FDI01SAV**. Its weight is not transferred across that code mismatch.
- Printed page 15 repeats **FD-31SVL** for a triple row, conflicting with the double row and the separate triple assembly in the spare-parts drawings. The existing double identity uses only its matching double row; no triple identity or weight is inferred from that apparent printing error.

All figures are manufacturer-listed values, not contributor measurements. No weights or limits are applied to older family revisions or different model codes merely because their group names match.
