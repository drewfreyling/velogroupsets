# Campagnolo 1980s weight sources

Campagnolo’s period publications need review by edition and model. Numbered catalogues 17/17a are 1970s publications, while the separate US Olympic catalogue reviewed here prints **copyright 1982**. Catalogue n.18 bis covers the later C-Record, Victory and Triomphe ranges. A weight from one range is not a reference for a similarly named later component.

## Reviewed 1982 Olympic catalogue

The [original manufacturer scan archived by Campy Only](https://www.campyonly.com/history/catalogs/1982_olympic_catalog.pdf) has 50 PDF pages. The Campagnolo branding, Campagnolo-USA publisher details, copyright and Form 989 appear in the book. Its [archive index](https://www.campyonly.com/history/catalogs.html) identifies this as the 1982 Olympic catalogue. The component facts below were checked against the scanned pages, rather than taken from archive commentary or a retailer listing.

| Model | Period family | Manufacturer-listed weight | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0102018 | Super Record | 182 g | PDF p. 14 / printed p. 12 |
| 1020/A | Nuovo Record | 199 g | Same table |
| 3500 | Nuovo Gran Sport | 220 g | Same table |

The table labels its weight row **“Weight Grams”**. It identifies the Super Record’s previous number **4001** alongside the newer **0102018**; these are one record, not two components. The Nuovo Record 1020/A and later Gran Sport 3500 remain distinct identities. The [December 1986 printing of catalogue n.18](https://ebykr.com/library/campagnolo-catalogue-18/) explicitly calls 3500 the **Nuovo Gran Sport** gear, corroborating the family identity; it is not assigned to the earlier 1950s Gran Sport group.

PDF pages 6–8 / printed pages 4–6 establish the Super Record, Nuovo Record and Gran Sport road-group memberships. Their existing family start years stay unchanged: these component records describe the **1982 reference offering**, rather than dating the families or every revision to 1982.

The rear-derailleur table also gives an unqualified **“Teeth Capacity”** row: 28 for 0102018 and 26 for 1020/A and 3500. It does not clearly distinguish total chain-wrap capacity from largest sprocket size or independently establish the other limits required by this catalogue. All four specific derailleur limits therefore remain null. No limits are inferred from cage appearance or another model.

## Later 1980s references and outstanding work

The already reviewed April 1985 n.18 bis scans establish C-Record, Victory and Triomphe identities. Its printed page 53 has a weight table for **rims**. That table does not supply weights for the 41 component identities in the later-1980s expansion. No qualifying weight reference for those records was found in that edition or the selected 1987/1989 brochures; their weights remain unknown.

The 1982 book also contains front-derailleur weights, but the applicable mounting and group variant must be resolved before adding a record. The unnamed Super Record front changer in the weight table is not given an invented standalone model code. Its brakeset codes include controls, so an overall brakeset figure would not automatically become a caliper-only or lever-pair weight. Hub weights require explicit skewer exclusion, and cranksets require length and chainrings without a bottom bracket.

Annual trade price/range sheets are useful leads, but each needs an identifiable year, original manufacturer provenance and an exact model/configuration reference before a weight is accepted. This review does not claim that all annual sheets from 1980–1989 have been located, or that every numbered parts catalogue contains complete component-weight tables.

## Narrow archive exception

Source review for this request on **14 November 2026** covers exactly the original manufacturer PDF at:

```text
https://www.campyonly.com/history/catalogs/1982_olympic_catalog.pdf
```

The catalogue validator accepts that exact HTTPS host/path for Campagnolo technical documents, including page fragments. Query strings, credentials, nonstandard ports, lookalike hosts and other Campy Only files remain rejected. This extends the [documented archive policy](campagnolo-record.md#approved-archived-manufacturer-documents) by one reviewed publication; it does not approve an entire archive or alter normal manufacturer-domain checks.
