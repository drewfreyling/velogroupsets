# Shimano component weight review

On 14 November 2026, the requested **146 model/variant records** were checked against selected period dealer/consumer catalogues and Shimano specification handbooks. **19 previously null weights** now have usable references; **127 remain null**. This is a record of the selected evidence and remaining gaps, not a claim that every page of every linked catalogue has been exhausted.

All additions are manufacturer-listed figures, not contributor measurements. Existing model IDs, memberships and contributor measurements are preserved. See the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md), [rear-derailleur review](shimano-rear-derailleur-weights.md) and [front-derailleur review](shimano-front-derailleur-weights.md).

## Source selection and conflicts

- **FC-5700:** the 2017 TSS p. 345 explicitly labels the reference “170 mm, w/o BB”: 756 g for 53/39T and 750 g for 52/39T. The existing 53/39T reference is retained at 756 g. This resolves the earlier BB-inclusive withholding; it does not justify deducting a BB from other cranks.
- **Power-meter cranks:** the 2025–2026 handbook gives 685 g for FC-R9100-P and 745 g for FC-R9200-P, both 170 mm, 50/34T without BB. The older 2017 FC-R9100-P table instead gives 679 g for that gearing. The current record selects the later edition without averaging or inventing a reason for the change.
- **BB-RS500:** the 2025–2026 specification book gives 92 g threaded and 71 g press-fit BB-RS500-PB. The 2017 TSS p. 347 repeats 71 g under both. The selected numeric weight uses the unambiguous later specification-book column; the thread variant within the threaded model remains unspecified.
- **Cassettes:** CS-HG50-8 is 278 g for U-group eight-speed 12–23T in the 2007 TSS, with both mass and tooth sequence on p. 223. Later blank cells do not invalidate that earlier, identified reference. CS-4600 is 310 g for 12–28T, not the separately listed 329 g 12–30T. CS-R8100-12 and CS-R8101-12 each have their own 291 g 11–30T entry; neither is inferred from the other. Their 11–34T entries are 345 g. The requested CS-HG70 seven-speed I-group remains unresolved because the earlier 1991 review found conflicting 286 g / 325 g figures for the selected gearing.
- **Hubs:** the 2009 road catalogue separately lists HB-7900 125 g / front QR 59 g and FH-7900 254 g / rear QR 64 g. The hub values are used directly. No skewer mass is subtracted. HB-RS770 and FH-RS770 are factory 12 mm through-axle hubs, at 100 mm and 142 mm O.L.D. respectively. The 2021 marketing page is PDF p. 139 but actually numbered **186**; the citation preserves both numbers.
- **Axle exclusions:** later tables list HB-9000 120 g, HB-6800 158 g, FH-9000 247 g and FH-6800 330 g under headings which also exclude an axle. Likewise HB-7710’s front/rear figures appear under an axle/lockring exclusion heading, despite an explicit rear-lockring inclusion. Those unresolved assembly bases remain null; the marketing repetitions without explicit skewer exclusions do not settle the wording.
- **Pedals and seatposts:** PD-7900 is explicitly a 248 g pair in the 2010–2011 specification table; the 2012 TSS separately lists 70 g cleats. SP-7200 identifies the 27.2 mm diameter. SP-7410 identifies 270 mm length but not which offered diameter supplied its 195 g average, and the description keeps that limitation.
- **Calipers:** no new complete-pair or position-specific figure with a qualified basis was established in this pass. BR-6700’s 2012 marketing weight does not explicitly establish the pair basis, and BR-5700’s checked tables do not resolve it. Short-reach weights are not copied to the requested 57-type variants.
