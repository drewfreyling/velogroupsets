# Shimano 1991 Exage LX and Deore DX follow-up

Reviewed on **24 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 1991 Shimano Bicycle System Component Dealer Product Manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf). The PDF has **88 pages**. PDF page numbers are three higher than the printed numbers in the selected tables; citations record both.

This follow-up adds **18 components**, enriches **three existing component records** and publishes **eight newly sourced weights**. It also moves the documented availability references for **Exage 500LX M500, 400LX M400 and 300LX M300 from 1992 to 1991**. These remain availability references, not asserted launch years. No groupsets are added. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, **277 groupsets and 2,085 components**.

Existing positive masses, IDs, model numbers and primary technical documents are preserved. The existing ST-M050 receives its first qualified pair weight; its previous unknown weight is not treated as a conflicting measured value.

The [1991 400LX table](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=72) resolves the ST-M050 qualification gap left by the [1992 review](shimano-1992-dealer.md). It prints both the exact model and the four-finger pair basis. No side is doubled, and the mass is not assigned to the three-finger **ST-M050-S**.

The three crank rows explicitly identify 170 mm arms and 48/38/28T, with bottom-bracket weights listed separately. Their canonical masses exclude bottom brackets without subtraction. The 175 mm arms remain available options without inheriting the selected mass. FC-M500's weighed ring profile is not specified; its alternative ring profiles are not all assigned the same weight.

The 1991 model cells say **FC-M500, FC-M400 and FC-M300**, while the 1992 cells explicitly use **-SG** suffixes. These records preserve the actual printed identities and cite their respective editions; this review does not assert that the names are interchangeable or replace the already published -SG records. Likewise, **SL-M453** remains distinct from the existing **SL-M453-A** reference. Its weight is read directly from its own row rather than copied from the suffixed model.

## Exage LX inventory and dates

The named tables at [PDF 71 / printed 68](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=71), [PDF 72 / printed 69](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=72) and [PDF 73 / printed 70](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=73) establish all three Exage LX ranges in 1991. Their start references and generation notes are revised accordingly; the 1992 citations remain as subsequent evidence.

SL-M300-B and SL-M300-C preserve the manufacturer's **Separate type / Unit type** labels and seven-speed indexing. Their records represent complete left/right references with unknown weights; no SL-M453 mass or unprinted front-speed configuration is assigned.

The new cantilevers BR-M454, BR-M351 and BR-M250 represent complete front/rear pairs. Their source figures do not explicitly establish all required pad/bolt inclusions, so their weights remain unknown rather than importing an incomplete assembly figure.

## Deore DX expansion

The [DX control table, PDF 59 / printed 56](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=59), supplies exact **ST-M070 four-finger / ST-M071 two-finger** models, seven-speed indexing and 22.2 mm clamps. The selected source's 535 g figure is assigned to ST-M071 but does not explicitly establish a pair basis. Both complete-pair records therefore retain unknown masses; the figure is not doubled or transferred to ST-M070.

The [brake/shift table, PDF 60 / printed 57](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=60), adds **BR-M650 super-low-profile**, **BR-M651 low-profile** and **BR-MT62** cantilevers. Each receives an unknown complete-pair mass pending explicit pad/bolt inclusion; BR-M651 does not inherit a neighbouring model's weight.

The [lever table, PDF 61 / printed 58](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1991_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf#page=61), adds the **BL-MT62 four-finger** pair with unknown weight. Its explicitly printed **274 g pair** figure belongs to **BL-MT63**, so the existing BL-MT63 record gains DX membership and retains its known mass. The existing **SL-MT62** likewise gains the explicitly documented DX membership while preserving its 232 g pair reference from the earlier source. Shared descriptions are made neutral without changing identities or primary documents.

These six new records and two shared memberships expand **Deore DX from 11 to 19 components**.

## Earlier reviews and remaining limits

The [21 November review](catalogue-review-2026-11-21.md#shimano-1991-dealer-manual) already captured the XT/DX/LX cage-specific rear weights and SL-M453-A pair. The [1990 MTB review](shimano-mtb-1990.md) already qualified selected normal-band front weights, bottom brackets and pedals. Those selected references are retained; broader clamp lists in this later edition do not silently redefine their weighed configurations.

This pass publishes no hub weights or undocumented quick-release deductions. Existing BR-M352 rear-only configuration, HB-RM50-F contributor measurement, conditional rear limits and all known primary references remain unchanged. U-brake position/assembly definitions, remaining XT/LX controls, 200GS/100GS inventories and additional road rows remain further review material. This is a targeted follow-up, not a claim that every page or figure in the manual has been imported.
