# Shimano 1992 Exage LX inventory and weight review

Reviewed on **23 November 2026** from the original [manufacturer-hosted 1992 Shimano Bicycle System Component Dealer Product Manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1992_Shimano_Bicycle_System_Component_Dealer_Product_Manual.pdf). The PDF has **108 pages**. The reviewed tables are **PDF 33 / printed 30** (Exage 500LX), **PDF 34 / printed 31** (400LX) and **PDF 35 / printed 32** (300LX); PDF fragments use the actual PDF page.

This pass adds **three MTB groupsets, 26 components and eight sourced weights**, and enriches **six existing shared records**. Exage 500LX M500 has **17 components**, 400LX M400 has **18**, and 300LX M300 has **17**. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, **277 groupsets and 2,067 components**. Existing IDs, model numbers, primary technical documents and known weights are preserved.

## Ranges and dated evidence

The three original tables explicitly name **Exage 500LX**, **Exage 400LX** and **Exage 300LX**, with their M500/M400/M300 component families. Each range uses **1992 as its first reviewed dated availability reference**, not an asserted launch year; the generation notes state that limitation.

These are MTB ranges, separate from the existing road Exage EX offerings and the later reviewed **Exage ES M520 / Exage LT M320**. The 1992 names do not replace those 1993 names, and model numbers are not treated as aliases merely because their ranges occupy similar positions.

## Eight manufacturer-listed weights

| RD-M500-SGS | 329 g | PDF 33 / printed 30, explicitly SGS |
| RD-M400-SGS | 330 g | PDF 34 / printed 31, explicitly super-long cage |
| RD-M300-SGS | 319 g | PDF 35 / printed 32, explicitly super-long cage |
| FC-M500-SG | 756 g | PDF 33 / printed 30: 170 mm, 48/38/28T |
| FC-M400-SG | 763 g | PDF 34 / printed 31: 170 mm, 48/38/28T, Biopace SG |
| FC-M300-SG | 756 g | PDF 35 / printed 32: 170 mm, 48/38/28T, Biopace SG |

| PD-M450 | 518 g per pair | PDF 33 / printed 30 explicitly prints pair |

Each crank weight is printed separately from the **339 g bottom-bracket assembly**, so no bottom-bracket mass is included in the canonical crank weight or removed by subtraction. Available 175 mm cranks are retained as options without receiving the selected 170 mm mass. The FC-M500-SG table offers Biopace and round configurations; the weight explicitly identifies 48/38/28T but does not identify the weighed ring profile, so its description states that limit rather than choosing one.

BB-M550's table lists 68/70 mm shell options, both with the same 122.5 mm spindle. Its weighed shell width is not specified. The distinct sealed **BB-CS20** retains an unknown weight and its documented 122.5/127.5 mm spindle options; BB-M550's mass is not transferred to it.

## Cage-specific limits

The manufacturer directly gives all four limits. No capacity or front difference is calculated from gear combinations.

| Models / cage | Total capacity | Largest sprocket | Smallest sprocket | Front difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M500 / M400 / M300 SGS | 38T | 32T | 12T | 20T |
| M500 / M400 / M300 GS | 36T | 30T | 12T | 20T |
| M400 SS | 28T | 28T | 12T | 13T |

All seven rear-derailleur identities retain explicit six-/seven-speed compatibility and select a seven-speed reference. Only the three SGS records receive masses. GS and SS weights remain unknown rather than inheriting the nearby SGS figure.

## Front derailleurs and brakes

The named **band-type FD-M500-B / FD-M400-B / FD-M300-B** and **EC-type FD-M500 / FD-M400 / FD-M300** remain separate identities. Their tables explicitly describe band diameters for both types, so they are recorded as clamp models. EC is retained as the manufacturer's type label, without reinterpreting it as a modern direct mount or bottom-bracket E-type assembly.

Band-type options are **28.0–28.6 / 30.0 / 31.8 mm**. EC-type FD-M500 additionally lists **34.9 mm**, while EC-type FD-M400 / FD-M300 list **28.0–28.6 / 30.0 / 34.9 mm** without a 31.8 mm option. Those differences are preserved. The separate **FD-M301 pull-top** model is added without assigning the neighbouring band-type mass or clamp options.

The six band/EC models record the directly printed **24T BioPace HP / 26T round-chainring capacities** separately. Their unqualified band-type weights do not identify an actual weighed band and remain unpublished; no EC or pull-top mass is inferred.

**BR-M501 M-arm** and **BR-M502 L-arm** are separate 500LX cantilever references. **BR-M201** is shared by 400LX and 300LX. Each uses a complete front/rear pair reference with unknown weight pending explicit pad/bolt inclusion. **BR-M352** is explicitly a **rear-only short-frame** option in the 400LX table and uses a rear reference, with no invented front counterpart or pair mass. The BR-M453 U-brake is left for a later review of its position and assembly definition.

## Controls and shared identities

The tables identify **ST-M050 four-finger** and **ST-M050-S three-finger** combined controls across all three ranges, with seven-speed indexing and 22.2 mm clamps. Both records represent complete left/right pairs. The printed four-finger figure does not establish a complete-pair basis in these selected tables, so no weight is published, doubled or transferred to the three-finger version. A separately specified front-control configuration is not invented.

Existing **FH-HG50**, **HB-RM50-F**, **CS-HG50 I-group 13–23T**, **HP-R500** and **HP-R501** gain all three explicitly documented memberships. **PD-M350** gains 400LX/300LX membership and keeps its existing 445 g pair reference. Shared parts are linked rather than duplicated, and their existing known masses and primary documents remain unchanged.

The HB-RM50-F contributor measurement remains **139 g for the 36-hole hub without its skewer**. The manufacturer's larger hub figures include or do not clearly exclude quick releases and are not used or reduced by estimated skewer masses. PD-M450 is kept separate from the road **PD-A450** model.

## Further review

This is a focused Exage LX inventory pass, not a complete audit of all 108 pages. The separately named 70GS/100GS/200GS offerings, additional XTR/XT/DX/LX variants, road tables and conditional brake configurations remain further review material. Hybrid CX systems are not forced into the existing road/MTB/gravel/track categories. An older edition's differing weight is not silently substituted for an existing selected reference.
