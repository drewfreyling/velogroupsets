Every selected cell explicitly says **/pair**. No side is doubled, no pair is divided and no mass is transferred between similarly named models. PD-ES600’s **single-sided binding** describes the usable binding face on each pedal, not a single-pedal weight.

## Axles and accessory limitations

PD-M9100 has two separate weight references: **316 g for short axles** and **318 g for normal axles**. The record explicitly selects the latter. Its [manufacturer drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/PD-M9100-4385/EV-PD-M9100-4385.pdf) identifies separate left/right normal and 3 mm shorter axle assemblies. No short-axle mass is applied to the normal-axle reference.

The specification notes for all seven selected models mention a supplied **1.0 mm cleat spacer**. The records preserve that note while stating that the weight row does not enumerate cleats or loose accessories. A package-inclusion row is not silently interpreted as a complete weighed-accessory inventory; no accessory deduction or addition is made.

For PD-M821, the handbook describes **four height-adjustable pins** but does not identify the exact pin-height setting for its published average. The record retains that limitation. Its [exact model drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/PD-M821-4729/EV-PD-M821-4729.pdf) identifies the pin/spacer assembly. PD-M821 remains distinct from PD-M820; this figure does not resolve the [earlier PD-M820 conflicting references](shimano-spd-pedal-weights.md).

PD-M8100-UG is a separately named edition in both the handbook and [shared drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/PD-M8100-4514/EV-PD-M8100-4514A.pdf). This pass adds the standard PD-M8100 record only. Existing PD-M540, PD-M520 and other pedal records keep their earlier selected references even where the newer handbook prints a different average.
