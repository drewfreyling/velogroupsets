# Shimano rear-derailleur weight review

On 14 November 2026, the requested **52 model/cage records** were checked against selected period catalogues and specification handbooks. This fills **11 previously null weights**; **41 stay null**. Blank/dash cells are missing data, not zero grams or proof that a model was never weighed.

The RD-M610-GS reference is the standard bracketed assembly. The handbook offers a bracketless specification separately; its mass is not inferred. RD-R8150 likewise uses the listed standard assembly. The 2021 tables omit M610-GS mass, but the older 2014–2015 handbook and 2017 TSS p. 285 explicitly provide 299 g.

RD-6200’s 190 g reference is the short cage, not the separate long-cage RD-6210. RD-6207’s 195 g is also the short cage; the 1987 page independently lists RD-6207-GS at 217 g. That long-cage model is outside the requested record and is not merged into it.

The 2009 Road consumer catalogue p. 7 prints **116 g** under RD-7900. This conflicts with the **166 g** reference in the manufacturer-hosted 2012 trade manual p. 121. The canonical record selects the latter; it does not silently correct, average or adopt the conflicting consumer figure.

The 2006 table also lists 265 g for RD-3300-SS and RD-4400-SS. Their existing **266 g** and **264 g** contributor measurements remain unchanged. RD-A551-SS’s **273 g** contributor measurement is also preserved. A published reference and a weighed example are different evidence.

## Review scope and source handling

The [dealer-manual index](shimano-dealer-manuals.md) supplies the earlier TSS links. Selected older scans from 1990, 1992, 1993, 1995, 1997 and 1998 were searched for period model tables, alongside the 1987 original manufacturer scan and later 2000/2001, 2006–2009, 2012/2013 and 2017/2018/2020/2021 references. Newer 2014–2015, 2017–2018 and 2025–2026 specification handbooks supplement those TSS editions. The June 1984 component book supplies the two early 600 references. Model hits and numbers found by OCR were checked against the relevant table images before adopting weights.

An indexed or searched publication is not a claim that all its pages were visually audited. Exact adopted source pages are cited above and in each canonical record. Manufacturer scans hosted by archives are weight evidence; existing official Shimano service/specification links remain the component technical documents. No new technical-document archive exception is introduced.

The [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md) still apply. Do not fill the remaining gaps with retailer figures, a neighbouring column, a later model, a cage conversion, a rounded guess or an undocumented measurement. A later contribution can supply a properly identified manufacturer reference or an explicitly attributed measurement.
