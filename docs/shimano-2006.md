# Shimano 2006 MTB dealer-manual follow-up

The owner supplied the [2006 Trade Sales Support Manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2006_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf). This partial review on **22 November 2026** expands the earlier road-weight review with the MTB specification tables on **PDF/printed pages 182–189**. The cached manufacturer PDF has 240 pages. Numerical facts were checked visually against the scanned tables; OCR was used for navigation only.

This batch adds **37 component records and 36 manufacturer-listed weights** across existing groupsets. RD-M531-SGS retains an unknown weight. No existing component identity, weight, membership or primary technical document is changed. New records cite the exact manufacturer-hosted manual page. The catalogue now has 1,796 components across 264 groupsets and four manufacturers.

## Rear derailleurs

[Page 186](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2006_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=186) identifies XTR and XT variants; [page 187](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/2006_Trade_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=187) identifies Hone, LX and Deore. These references all have nine-speed rear compatibility, a 34T largest sprocket, 11T smallest sprocket and 22T maximum front difference.

| Added reference | Range | Total capacity | Weight |

| RD-M960-GS | XTR M960 | 33T | 198 g |

| RD-M580-GS | Deore LX M580 | 33T | 278 g |
| RD-M531-SGS | Deore M530 | 45T | Unknown |

The existing generic model IDs already select SGS references for RD-M960, RD-M760, RD-M600 and RD-M580; they are preserved and not duplicated. The new GS records have their own sourced weights and limits. The RD-M531-SGS weight cell is blank; the neighbouring RD-M511-SGS figure is not transferred. RD-M531 is not treated as an alias for the existing RD-M530 record. Older alternative models such as RD-M952, RD-M751 and RD-M571 are left for further generation-specific membership review.

## XTR disc calipers

Page 184 separately lists **BR-M965 front 98 g and rear 100 g**, with resin pads. The caliper-weight row explicitly includes fixing bolts. Two position-specific records retain the actual manufacturer model number BR-M965, International Standard mounting and two opposed pistons. They do not split a pair total or reuse a complete hydraulic-system figure.

## Remaining scope

- The crank tables on pages 182–183 include bottom brackets in their marked totals. No standalone crank weight is generated by subtraction.
- Clamp-front-derailleur weights remain subject to identifying the actual weighed band size. Available clamp options do not identify the weighed configuration.
- Other hydraulic calipers and complete systems require further pad, adapter, mounting-hardware and hose/configuration review.
- Existing road weights and contributor measurements remain unchanged. Earlier road reviews are linked in the [dealer-manual index](shimano-dealer-manuals.md).
- This is a selected MTB specification-table review, not an exhaustive audit of all 240 pages.
