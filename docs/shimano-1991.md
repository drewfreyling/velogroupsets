# 1991 Shimano dealer catalogue source review

The [1991 Shimano dealer catalogue scan hosted by Vintage Cannondale](https://vintagecannondale.com/cannondale/shimano/1991%20Shimano.pdf) is an 88-page image-only PDF. The figures below were checked against rendered pages, rather than accepted from OCR alone. PDF page numbers count from the cover; the printed page number is three lower for the cited component tables. Manufacturer-hosted Shimano service drawings remain each component’s technical-document link. The scan is a supplementary manufacturer weight/membership source, not a replacement technical-document host.

## Added reference weights

| Component | Weight | Source and weighed configuration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FC-7402 | 643 g | Printed p. 20 / PDF p. 23 identifies **FC-7402-SG**, 170 mm, 52/42T. The BB-7400 is listed separately at 292 g. This resolves the older scan’s standard/Biopace ambiguity for the selected SG reference. |
| BL-6403 | 272 g | Printed p. 29 / PDF p. 32 explicitly says **Pair**; aero levers, 23.8–24.2 mm clamp, 84 mm stroke. |
| HB-7600-F | 241 g | Printed p. 26 / PDF p. 29: large-flange front track hub, 100 mm over locknuts. The catalogue specifies hollow axles and shows hub nuts; Shimano’s front-hub exploded view confirms nut fastening, with no quick-release skewer. The spoke-hole reference is not identified. |
| HB-7600-R | 313 g | Same page: large-flange rear track hub, 120 mm over locknuts, including lockring; sprocket listed separately. It is nut-fastened, without a quick-release skewer. The spoke-hole reference is not identified. |
| HP-7600 | 90 g | Same page: track headset, 19.5 mm upper-cone and 11.5 mm lower-cone height; weighed thread variant unspecified. New component record, with its Shimano-hosted exploded view. |

The nut-fastened track hubs differ from quick-release road hubs: no skewer weight was deducted. The 1991 hollow-axle reference is stated explicitly rather than describing every HB-7600 revision as solid-axle. Other HB-7710 weights remain unsourced.

## 105 SC coverage and unresolved references

- **HB-1055-F is now listed.** Printed p. 36 / PDF p. 39 establishes its 105 SC membership and 32/36-hole, 96/100 mm over-locknut options. Its 216 g figure includes quick release, so its canonical weight remains null. The official exploded view shows the 100 mm version; the broader options come from the catalogue.
- **CS-HG70 seven-speed I-group is shared with RX100.** Printed p. 37 / PDF p. 40 lists it for 105 SC at **286 g**, including spacers, and identifies the 13-14-15-17-19-21-23T sequence. The RX100 table on printed p. 40 / PDF p. 43 lists the same model/group/range at **325 g**, agreeing with the older RX100 reference. The scan does not identify a variant explaining the difference. Both citations are retained and the single canonical weight is withheld rather than choosing a figure or inventing two component identities. Eight-speed CS-HG70-8 remains separate.
- **BB-1055 stays null.** Printed p. 36 / PDF p. 39 gives 340 g and lists 113/115 mm spindle options without selecting the weighed length. Neither option can be assumed.
- **FH-1055 stays null.** The same page explicitly labels 415 g with quick release. It is not a skewer-free hub weight. The later FH-1056 remains an eight-speed component and is not covered by this seven-speed table.
- **ST-7400 now uses the explicit 480 g pair reference in the 1994 manual, p. 42.** The earlier printed p. 17 / PDF p. 20 lists 560 g without an explicit left/right pair basis; that earlier figure remains unused. The downtube shifter tables likewise do not supply a new explicit pair-weight reference for the withheld Dura-Ace shifter figures.
- **FH-7402 stays null.** Printed p. 20 / PDF p. 23 explicitly gives 460 g with quick release for FH-7402-8, clarifying the earlier “without sprockets” wording.

The 1991 tables corroborate FC-1055’s 710 g 170 mm/52–42T crank-only reference, BL-1055’s 252 g pair, PD-1055’s 433 g pair with size-L toe clips, and PD-1056’s 460 g pair excluding separately listed cleats/hardware. Those already-sourced weights are not new measurements. HP-1055 remains 101 g; its existing 1990 dimension reference is retained rather than silently replacing its stated 15 mm lower-cone height with the 1991 table’s 11.5 mm figure.

Road and track weights were reviewed for existing catalogue models, alongside RX100 and Exage road tables. Mountain-bike groups in this scan are outside this update. Brake-caliper pair totals are now permitted by the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md#brake-calipers); the 1991 calipers remain to be reviewed and added under the [caliper contract](brake-calipers.md). Do not split their pair totals into estimated individual weights.
