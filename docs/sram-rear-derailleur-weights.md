# SRAM rear-derailleur weight review

The 28 requested SRAM rear-derailleur records were reviewed on **14 November 2026**. **27 previously null weights** now have identified reference configurations; **RD-APX-1-D1 remains null** because the available numerical fields conflict. No model IDs, memberships or existing derailleur limits were changed.

Manufacturer tables, manufacturer claims reported by publications/retailers, retailer specifications and independent sample figures are distinguished in each record. These are not all direct manufacturer measurements. See the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md) and [SRAM family guide](sram.md).

## Selected references

Electronic rear-derailleur figures below exclude the battery unless the row explicitly says **includes battery**. The original eleven-speed RED eTap measurement includes its battery and is not converted into an estimated battery-free value. Mechanical derailleurs have no battery. A reference cage or maximum sprocket identifies the weighed variant; it does not narrow every configuration covered by the component’s Model ID.

## Manufacturer weight sheets

The original SRAM-branded sheets are publicly preserved by a retailer/publication. They are supplementary weight citations; the manufacturer-hosted service/technical documents remain the component technical links. This does not expand any approved technical-document archive exception.

- [SRAM RED AXS Weight Overview, 2024](https://images.performancebike.com/images/resources/SRAMREDAXSGROUP-PB/Manuals/2-SRAM-RED-Weights.pdf): PDF p. 1 gives the **no-battery** methodology and identifies 36T-max RED AXS / Force AXS and 33T-max older RED/Force eTap AXS references. PDF p. 2 gives **262 g RED E1**, **278 g RED D1 (33T)** and **299 g Force D1 (33T)**. The PDF’s document metadata identifies a February 2024 file; its generic download filename is not an edition year.
- [SRAM Force and Rival Weight Overview, 2025](https://www.slowtwitch.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SR_ForceRival_AXS_WeightSheets_en_v05.pdf): PDF pp. 1–2 give no-battery road figures of **284 g Force E1**, **301 g Force D2**, **306 g Rival E1** and **342 g Rival D1**. Pages 3–4 give no-battery XPLR figures of **418 g Force E1 (13-speed)**, **308 g Force D2 (12-speed)** and **435 g Rival E1 (13-speed)**. Rival D1 XPLR’s 327 g also corroborates the support FAQ.
- [SRAM’s XPLR weight FAQ](https://support.sram.com/hc/en-us/articles/6014379628955-What-are-the-weights-of-the-XPLR-derailleurs-and-cassettes) explicitly gives **293 g RED**, **308 g Force**, **327 g Rival** and **323 g Apex**, all **without battery**. These are the twelve-speed, 44T-max hanger-mounted models; the older FAQ is not used for thirteen-speed Full Mount E1. Force D2’s separate 2025 sheet establishes its own 308 g reference rather than inferring equality from Force D1.

SRAM groupset totals include other parts and battery mass. No rear-derailleur mass is calculated by subtracting other components from a groupset total.

## Earlier mechanical and independent references

The original [2011 SRAM road catalogue](https://issuu.com/monzaimports/docs/sram_road_11), preserved in distributor Monza Imports’ public Issuu reader, has native text sections for viewer pp. **16 (RED)**, **20 (Force)**, **24 (Rival)** and **28 (Apex)**. Those sections give 153 g, 178 g, 188 g short cage and 200 g short cage respectively. The first two are the documented 28T-max period references; their weights are not extended to later WiFLi options. Apex’s separately listed 210 g mid-cage figure is not used. These are viewer-page numbers, not an inferred printed-page offset.

The record’s additional references identify their actual assemblies:

- **RD-FRC-1-A1:** The Pro’s Closet lists a legacy Force 1 medium-cage Type 2 used sample at **261 g**, with cable pinch bolt and both pulleys included. This is a retailer-listed sample reference, not a claimed average for every Force 1 revision. A conflicting 261 g long-cage retail listing is not adopted; a later Type 3.0 long-cage listing gives 271 g. No weight is transferred to an A2 model or calculated from Rival’s weight difference.
- **RD-APX-1-A1:** Velo’s February 2016 Apex 1 launch reports the **314 g** manufacturer claim and the original long-cage 42T-max, eleven-speed assembly. It is not the newer mechanical twelve-speed XPLR model.
- **RD-RIV-1-A1:** Cycling Weekly explicitly reports the manufacturer claim of **269 g for the medium cage**. No subtraction from this figure is used to supply Force’s weight.
- **RD-FRC-B1 / RD-RED-B1 / RD-RED-C2:** the selected retailer tables identify the exact ten/eleven-speed system, short cage and 28T-max reference. The RED C2 table separately gives 165 g for its medium cage. The matching 145 g short-cage figures for RED B1/C2 are separately sourced, not copied between generations.
- **RD-RIV-B1:** Disraeli Gears documents its **179 g** short-cage RIV-B1 sample, including 55 mm pulley-centre spacing. It is an independent sample figure, distinct from generic 180 g retail claims. Its unverified “bikeshop folklore” capacity statement is not adopted.
- **RD-RED-E-A1:** Ben Delaney’s January 2016 BikeRadar report gives **237 g including the battery**, and separately quotes SRAM’s 239 g claim. The catalogue selects the published complete measurement. The source explains that its photographs show the derailleur with a red travel cover and without the battery; the catalogue uses the author’s stated complete-assembly result, not a mass read from those photographs or an undocumented deduction.
- **RD-RED-1E-E1:** BIKE24 reports a manufacturer-sourced **375 g** figure for exact Model ID RD-RED-1E-E1 / part 00.7518.185.000, supplied without battery. This retailer-reported reference remains distinct from the primary SRAM FAQ’s 293 g twelve-speed D1 assembly.

## Unresolved mechanical Apex XPLR

[RD-APX-1-D1](../data/components/sram-rd-apx-1-d1.json) remains **null**. [Wheelproject’s exact-model listing](https://wheelproject.com/product/sram-apex-xplr-rear-derailleur-12-speed-44t/) identifies part **00.7518.177.000**, gives **288 g** in a technical field labelled manufacturer weight, and separately lists **372 g** in additional information without explaining that field. Other description text also mixes in AXS material. This review does not assume the larger figure is packaging or infer a corrected mass. Manufacturer/service pages and other checked mechanical listings establish the model but did not resolve the numerical reference. The figures are retained as research leads, not a canonical weight.

## Model options and limits

Weight-reference fields were added, and the previously absent Rival 1 speed field now identifies its documented eleven-speed system. Existing `cage_variants`, `largest_sprocket_variants` and all four sourced-or-null derailleur limits are preserved. In particular, a 33T-max reference weight does not turn the RED/Force D1 family’s universal maximum into 33T or 36T, and a short-cage measurement does not establish total capacity for the WiFLi version. `includes_battery` records the electronic weight inclusion; the description always states it in plain language.
