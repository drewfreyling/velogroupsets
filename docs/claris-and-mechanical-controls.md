# Claris calipers and mechanical control weights

Reviewed on 19 November 2026. The Claris R2000 inventory was missing BR-R2000. The existing 105 R7000 and Ultegra R8000 inventories already had sourced weights for their listed cranks, derailleurs, cassettes, pedals and rim calipers; their remaining weight gaps were ST-R7000 and ST-R8000 mechanical control pairs.

## Claris BR-R2000

The [2017–2018 line-up, PDF page 93](https://productinfo.shimano.com/pdfs/product/archive/2017-2018_Line-up_chart_v024_en.pdf#page=93) places BR-R2000 in the Claris R2000 family. The [2025–2026 specification handbook, PDF page 153](https://productinfo.shimano.com/pdfs/product/archive/2025-2026_Specifications_v035_en.pdf#page=153) identifies its front/rear mounting hardware, NEW SUPER SLR dual-pivot design, 51 mm reach and R50T5 standard shoes in that edition. The average-weight cell is blank. Other online editions list different standard shoes; the record preserves the cited edition rather than combining their specifications.

[BR-R2000](../data/components/shimano-br-r2000.json) is one complete front/rear pair record. Its weight remains explicitly unknown pending a sourced complete-pair figure or compliant contributor measurement with pads and mounting bolts. It does not inherit BR-R3000's identity or any other caliper's weight.

## Ultegra ST-R8000: 438 g per pair

The original [Japanese Shimano 2020 Bike Gear catalogue, PDF page 7](https://cp-wheel.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/2020%E3%82%B7%E3%83%9E%E3%83%8E.pdf#page=7) lists ST-R8000 under the explicit heading **平均重量（ペア）**, meaning **average weight (pair)**, at **438 g**. Its row identifies the 2×11-speed left/right set and both individual-hand order codes. This is a directly published pair figure, not a sum of the separate 220 g and 218 g figures in the English handbook.

The catalogue lists shift and brake cables among the set's package contents, but does not separately establish whether those cables are included in its reference weight. The weight description preserves that limitation. The existing manufacturer-hosted ST-R8000 exploded view remains the primary technical document, and the existing canonical pair ID and memberships remain unchanged.

### Archived publication reviewed

The exact CP Wheel PDF linked above is an archived **original Shimano publication**, not a retailer product listing. Its cover identifies the Bike Gear catalogue; its contents page dates the information to 5 March 2020, and its back cover identifies Shimano's customer-service contact and manufacturer website. The Ultegra table and its pair heading were checked visually because the PDF's embedded fonts do not extract all model numbers correctly. Only its original manufacturer table supports this weight. The PDF remains external; it is not copied into Git. This supporting citation does not widen the technical-document archive exception.

## 105 ST-R7000: calculated 500 g pair weight

The [2025–2026 specification handbook, PDF page 136](https://productinfo.shimano.com/pdfs/product/archive/2025-2026_Specifications_v035_en.pdf#page=136) gives **251 g for ST-R7000-R** and **249 g for ST-R7000-L**. The [2019 trade manual, PDF page 387](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/2019_TRADE_SALES_SUPPORT_MANUAL.pdf#page=387) likewise labels these as individual-side figures. The Japanese 2020 catalogue's R7000 control table on PDF page 9 leaves its pair-weight column blank.

The owner approved adding the two manufacturer-published side figures on 19 November 2026. Under the revised [shifter weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md#shifters), the canonical ST-R7000 pair weight is **500 g**, calculated as **249 g left + 251 g right**. The record labels this calculation and retains both exact model codes and side weights. It is a calculated total of published individual averages, rather than a directly published pair figure or a new measurement. ST-R8000 retains its directly published 438 g pair reference.

## Existing R7000/R8000 caliper weights

BR-R7000 already has a **379 g front/rear pair** reference; BR-R8000 has separately sourced **182 g front** and **178 g rear** records. These include standard pads and mounting bolts, with the mounting-nut/bolt length left unspecified where the source does not identify it. They are preserved rather than duplicated or replaced by calculated pair/individual figures. See the [brake-caliper source notes](brake-calipers.md).
