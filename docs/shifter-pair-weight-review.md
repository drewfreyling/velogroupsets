# Shifter pair weight review

The later [1997–2000 Shimano MTB control review](shimano-mtb-controls-1997-2000.md) fills the SL-M750, SL-M570 and SL-M510 pair weights and adds distinct ST combined-control pairs. ST and SL model codes remain separate identities.

Reviewed on 19 November 2026 after the [shifter weight rule](weights-guidelines.md#shifters) began allowing separately manufacturer-published left and right weights to be added for matching controls.

The audit started with 113 shifter records without weights: 100 pairs and 13 genuine single controls. The latter remain single controls; this rule does not supply a missing measurement for them. Eleven existing pairs (nine Shimano and two microSHIFT) now have calculated weights, leaving 102 unknown shifter weights (89 pairs and 13 singles). Known pair totals and canonical component identities are preserved.

## Newly calculated pairs

Each record retains both side model references, both published side weights, the sum, and a citation to the manufacturer table. These are calculated reference totals, not measurements of an assembled pair.

SL-M8000, SL-M7000-11 and SL-M610 use band-clamp references, not I-SPEC weights. SL-M7000-11 uses the manufacturer's shared SL-M7000-L front control, rather than inventing a left model with an `-11` suffix. The Di2 switch tables explicitly publish both hands, even where their weights happen to match; neither side is inferred by doubling the other. SW-M9050 uses the selected 2017–2018 figures rather than mixing them with older handbook averages. ST-RX820 excludes the different ST-RX820-LA dropper control. Hydraulic lever weights cover the controls, not complete lever/hose/caliper systems.

microSHIFT publishes both hands under the same pair model number. Their side references therefore qualify the real model with `(left)` and `(right)`, rather than inventing manufacturer suffixes. SB-G7000 includes its brake-only left lever; SB-G7020 includes the two shifting controls. The manufacturer does not specify cable/housing inclusion.

## Remaining gaps

The remaining unknown weights are 50 Shimano, 26 Campagnolo, 21 SRAM and five microSHIFT records. Reviewing the available dealer/specification handbooks and existing manufacturer references did not establish further matching left/right figures for these records. This is an audit of available references, not a claim that no other publication contains them.

- **Shimano:** the original SL-M980 drawing identifies the original revision. Later handbook figures for SL-M980-A and SL-M980-B describe different revisions and mounts, so neither total is transferred. Other older controls still need a published pair total or both exact side figures.
- **SRAM:** the [previous component review](sram-component-weights.md) includes hydraulic-system figures with calipers, hoses or fluid. They cannot become standalone control weights by addition or subtraction. A two-chainring Rival 22 pair total also cannot supply the one-chainring SB-RIV-B1 / BL-RIV-A1 pair, whose left lever is brake-only. No new complete set of matching control-only side figures was established.
- **microSHIFT:** the [2017 catalogue review](microshift.md) found numerical technical-table entries for double-chainring controls, without establishing weights for the three exact triple variants. Adding cannot resolve that configuration mismatch. The unknown single controls still need exact weight references.
- **Campagnolo:** the reviewed publications did not establish additional exact left/right figures for the unknown pairs. Existing explicitly published pair totals remain unchanged.

Unknown-record explanations now describe these source/configuration gaps rather than prohibiting side-weight addition. The existing ST-R7000 calculated 500 g pair and ST-R8000 published 438 g pair are unchanged.
