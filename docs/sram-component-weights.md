# SRAM front derailleurs, cranksets, controls and cassette weights

The **69 requested model/pair records** were reviewed on **14 November 2026**. **39 previously null weights** now have reference configurations; **30 remain null**. All 13 requested cassettes have a selected weight. Existing model IDs, memberships and manufacturer technical links are preserved.

Sources distinguish original manufacturer tables, manufacturer claims reported by publications/retailers, published measurements and retailer specifications. Original SRAM weight sheets preserved elsewhere are supplementary citations; they do not change the manufacturer-domain rule for technical documents. See the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md) and the earlier [rear-derailleur review](sram-rear-derailleur-weights.md).

## Source interpretation

- **2024 RED AXS sheet:** [methodology and configurations on PDF p. 1](https://images.performancebike.com/images/resources/SRAMREDAXSGROUP-PB/Manuals/2-SRAM-RED-Weights.pdf#page=1), mass table on p. 2. Front derailleurs are braze-on with wedge, bolt and washer, without batteries. Cranks use 172.5 mm / 48/35T; bottom brackets are separate. Non-power crank rows are selected, not the heavier power-meter rows. Older RED/Force eTap AXS and current RED/Force AXS are separate named references.
- **2025 Force/Rival sheet:** [road methodology/table on PDF pp. 1–2](https://www.slowtwitch.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SR_ForceRival_AXS_WeightSheets_en_v05.pdf#page=1), XPLR on pp. 3–4. Road cranks use 172.5 mm / 48/35T; XPLR cranks use 172.5 mm / 40T. BBs are separate. The selected non-power figures are 622 g Force E1, 801 g Rival E1, 530 g Force E1 XPLR and 722 g Rival E1 XPLR. These sourced lengths are accepted without normalising them to the preferred 170 mm.
- **Model identity matters:** the retained XG-1250-D1 cassette is listed for Rival E1 as well as D1, while Force E1 has its distinct XG-1270-E1 record. Power-meter crank assemblies are not silently assigned to the corresponding non-power crank reference. The old RED double weight is not transferred to a 1x model.
- **Mount and battery:** the original RED eTap front reference is BikeRadar’s reported 162 g including battery, in its pictured braze-on assembly without a clamp adapter. The published complete figure is used directly; no battery or travel-cover calculation is performed here. Force AXS Wide’s published 156 g excludes battery, and the pictured weighed assembly has no clamp adapter. Standard-chainline weights are not copied to Wide records.
- **Chain spotters:** the Force 22 retailer specification gives 79 g plus a separate 10 g when its spotter is fitted; RED C2 gives 69 g with a separate 12 g spotter addition. The records select the explicitly listed base masses without spotters. No combined weight is calculated. The unresolved Rival 22 figure does not have an equally clear hardware mapping in the checked sources.
- **Pairs:** the original Rival ten-speed 320 g catalogue figure is paired with SRAM’s exact-model “Weight Based On: pair” specification. Force 22’s 307 g and RED 22’s 280 g are explicitly pair figures. The original eTap publication explicitly lists 260 g/pair with coin-cell batteries; no individual unit is doubled or sides summed by the catalogue. Cable/housing inclusion remains unspecified.
- **Hydraulics:** SRAM’s sheet “Shift-Brake System” totals include calipers, hoses, fluid, fixings and batteries as described. The current ED-RED-E1 service field likewise includes the complete hydraulic system. Those figures cannot supply a control-only pair weight. A packaged pair of brake-only Rival 1 levers is also not the named mixed shift/brake pair.
- **Cassettes:** SRAM’s [XPLR FAQ](https://support.sram.com/hc/en-us/articles/6014379628955-What-are-the-weights-of-the-XPLR-derailleurs-and-cassettes) gives PG-1231 425 g for 11–44T, XG-1251 412 g for 10–44T and XG-1271 373 g for 10–44T. The selected XG-1190-A2 151 g retailer reference is **11–28T**, not 11–25T. PG-1050 and PG-1130 retain the complete source sprocket sequences. Lockring inclusion follows the owner’s established convention without an extra verification requirement.

This is a review of the selected sources and explicit remaining gaps, not a claim that every historical publication has been exhausted.
