# Shimano 2002 STI service-table review

Reviewed on **22 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 2002 Shimano STI manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/2002_Shimano_STI.pdf). The PDF has **156 pages** and its archive disclaimer explicitly says the information was updated in **2002**. Some contents headings still say 2001; those headings are not used to relabel the publication or infer a component launch date.

This targeted pass adds **six components** and enriches **four existing records**. It adds no new groupsets or weights and preserves existing IDs, primary documents, start years and known masses. After this batch the catalogue contains four manufacturers, 271 groupsets and 1,939 components.

The [manufacturer's discontinued-products index](https://bike.shimano.com/en-AU/products/discontinued.html) explicitly classifies BB-7703 as Dura-Ace, BR-R600 and PD-6601 as Ultegra, PD-5500 as 105, and BR-M515 as Deore. Those classifications and the period service sheets support catalogue-era associations; recommended lever compatibility alone is not used as proof of group membership. BR-R600's compatibility with Dura-Ace and 105 levers does not add it to those groupsets.

BR-R600 retains the manufacturer's actual model number. It is a **47–57 mm reach** caliper, not a newly invented “-57” manufacturer suffix. Its pair weight remains unknown until a full front/rear reference with pads and mounting bolts is established. The pedal records likewise require a complete pair weight; no single pedal mass is doubled.

BB-7703 is identified separately from FC-7703. The service table does not supply a spindle-length/weight reference, and no neighbouring bottom bracket's dimensions or mass are transferred.

BR-M515's installation sheet shows its adapter and separately illustrates post-type fork mounting. It is recorded as a caliper, not a complete cable/lever/rotor kit. The alternative BR-M515-LA model remains a follow-up identity/membership review rather than automatically receiving the base model's group classification.

The explicit SM-RT60 diagram resolves a lead left by the [2003 review](shimano-2003-sti.md). Its mount is not inferred from SM-RT75, its 160 mm diameter comes from its own exact 2003 rotor-table entry, and it is not treated as an alias of SM-RT61. Hardware inclusion for a future weight remains unresolved.

## Page mapping and remaining leads

PDF and printed numbering do **not** have a single offset across this file: technical chart PDF 62 is printed 59, while service PDF 122 is printed 120. Every cited location was checked against the actual printed number and URL fragments use PDF page numbers.

BR-M755-DH's downhill instructions identify special front-fork adapters and an International Standard rear frame boss. That does not by itself identify every caliper-side mounting interface, so no generic native-mount claim is added. Hybrid/Nexave assemblies and additional wheel, hub and control sheets remain follow-up work. The selected sections do not publish any new qualified weights.
