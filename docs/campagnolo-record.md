# Campagnolo Record 8- and 9-speed

The catalogue has separate **Record 8-speed** and **Record 9-speed** family records under Campagnolo. Only a documented start year is recorded, and the manufacturer page lists the families oldest first.

| Family | Start year | Source basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record 8-speed | 1991 | Campagnolo’s *1992 Road Range*, published September 1991, lists the eight-speed Record range |
| Record 9-speed | 1997 | The *1997 Spare Parts Catalogue* documents separate Record eight- and nine-speed offerings |

The original manufacturer publications are preserved by Disraeli Gears: [1992 Road Range](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_-_1992_road_range.html), [1994 parts catalogue](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_spare_parts_catalogue_-_1994_product_range.html) and [1997 parts catalogue](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_spare_parts_catalogue_-_1997.html). The archive identifies the 1997 scans as coming from a PDF previously downloaded from Campagnolo’s website. A family’s start year does not date every later component revision to that year; eight-speed Ergopower and the eight-speed family’s first appearance are distinct events.

## Approved archived manufacturer documents

The project owner approved these archived manufacturer documents on 8 November 2026 because Campagnolo’s former 1990s PDF URLs now return 404 and its current [official documentation area](https://www.campagnolo.com/gb-en/technical-support-area/documentation/) does not list these editions. The exception covers original Campagnolo spare-parts pages hosted by Disraeli Gears: pages 1–58 of the 1994 catalogue and pages 1–88 of the 1997 catalogue. An additional explicit source review on 8 November 2026 covered four original Campagnolo brochures archived by Disraeli Gears: catalogue n.18 bis (April 1985, pages 1–56), Chorus (July 1987, scans 1–14), Croce d’Aune (November 1987, scans 1–12), and Athena (April 1989, scans 1–10). The original scanned group lists and component codes were checked for the [1980s expansion](campagnolo-groupsets.md#the-1980s-road-groups). Their exact page/scan paths are also permitted; index commentary is not a technical document. Other publishers, manufacturers, editions or archive paths are not automatically accepted.

The catalogue validator permits only HTTPS URLs on `disraeligears.co.uk` or `www.disraeligears.co.uk`, using those exact catalogue page and brochure scan paths, for the Campagnolo manufacturer whose official website is `campagnolo.com`. It rejects unrelated archive pages, lookalike domains, credentials, nonstandard ports and query strings. Normal manufacturer-hosted documents remain valid. The exception establishes reviewed provenance; the original manufacturer publication remains the source of the facts, rather than treating any archive commentary or retailer listing as a technical document.

## Components and reference edition

The catalogue includes **17 component identities across 25 memberships**, documenting the 1997 Record offering rather than claiming these revisions all launched with the eight-speed family in 1991.

| Component | Record 8-speed | Record 9-speed | 1997 catalogue page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rear derailleur | RD-08RE | RD-09RE | 24 / 22 |
| Ergopower lever pair | EC-08RECG | EC-09RECG | 40 / 38 |
| Standalone brake lever pair | BL-22RECG | BL-22RECG | 54 |
| Double crankset | FC-21RE | FC-21RE | 11 |
| Braze-on front derailleur | FD-31SRE | FD-31SRE | 31 |
| Bottom brackets | BB-31RECART / BB-33RECART | BB-31RECART / BB-33RECART | 8 |
| Threaded headset | HS-21RE | HS-21RE | 4 |
| Front hub | HB-30RE | HB-30RE | 57 |
| Rear freehub | FH-08RE | FH-09RE | 59 / 57 |
| Cassette | CS-008TI, titanium, 13–23T | CS-09RE, steel, or CS-09RETI, titanium, 12–23T | 65 / 62–63 |
| Seatpost | SP-10RE | SP-10RE | 67 |

Common Record parts retain one identity and both groupset memberships. The eight- and nine-speed rear derailleurs, Ergopower controls, rear freehubs and cassettes remain separate. Membership does not assert that all parts from the two systems are interchangeable, or that an earlier/later Record revision has identical specifications. The headset is specifically the 1997 HS-21RE revision.

The spare-parts drawings establish model identities and revisions but do not establish compliant component weights. All 17 weights remain null under the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md); rear-derailleur limits also remain unknown until sourced. No modern Record figures are applied retrospectively, and hub weights must exclude skewers. Brake calipers, chains and complete wheelsets remain outside the current taxonomy.

BL-22RECG is a separate standalone brake-lever pair, shared by both contemporary Record offerings and documented on approved archive page 54. Its weight remains null because the spare-parts page supplies no pair weight. See the [brake-lever source notes](brake-levers.md).
