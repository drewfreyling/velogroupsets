# Campagnolo Record 8- and 9-speed

The catalogue has separate **Record 8-speed** and **Record 9-speed** family records under Campagnolo. Only a documented start year is recorded, and the manufacturer page lists the families oldest first.

| Family | Start year | Source basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record 8-speed | 1991 | Campagnolo’s *1992 Road Range*, published September 1991, lists the eight-speed Record range |
| Record 9-speed | 1997 | The *1997 Spare Parts Catalogue* documents separate Record eight- and nine-speed offerings |

The original manufacturer publications are preserved by Disraeli Gears: [1992 Road Range](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_-_1992_road_range.html), [1994 parts catalogue](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_spare_parts_catalogue_-_1994_product_range.html) and [1997 parts catalogue](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_spare_parts_catalogue_-_1997.html). The archive identifies the 1997 scans as coming from a PDF previously downloaded from Campagnolo’s website. A family’s start year does not date every later component revision to that year; eight-speed Ergopower and the eight-speed family’s first appearance are distinct events.

## Component-document availability

Campagnolo’s former 1990s PDF URLs now return 404, and its current [official documentation area](https://www.campagnolo.com/gb-en/technical-support-area/documentation/) does not list these editions. The groupset records are published with sources; their component lists remain empty under the current rule requiring manufacturer-hosted technical documents. This is a documentation gap, not a claim that the original groups lacked parts.

A researched component draft covers 16 identities from the 1997 parts edition: distinct RD-08RE/RD-09RE rear derailleurs, EC-08RECG/EC-09RECG Ergopower controls, FH-08RE/FH-09RE rear hubs and eight-/nine-speed cassette models, plus shared Record crank, front derailleur, bottom brackets, headset, front hub and seatpost. Publishing that draft requires an explicitly approved exception for the archived manufacturer documents. The existing technical-document host rule has not been silently relaxed.

The spare-parts drawings establish model identities and revisions but do not establish compliant component weights. Draft weights remain null; rear-derailleur limits also remain unknown until sourced. No modern Record figures are applied retrospectively. Brakes, standalone brake levers, chains and complete wheelsets remain outside the current taxonomy.
