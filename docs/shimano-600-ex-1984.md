# Shimano 600 EX and 600 AX: June 1984 technical catalogue

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 from the [original manufacturer catalogue scan supplied by the project owner](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1sLOMGjcPBzkKLw70IicwaOOS7qI4zAAn/view). The scan has 162 PDF pages; its final page states copyright June 1984 and print code `0684 FC/10M`. The technical pages checked below have printed page numbers one lower than the PDF page number.

This batch adds **eight component records**, fills **two existing weight gaps** and records **six newly sourced weights** overall. The catalogue now contains 1,443 components. Existing IDs, slugs, model numbers, groupset memberships and primary technical documents remain unchanged. New 6207-series records belong to the existing **600 EX 6207** range, distinct from the earlier 600 EX 6200 and the 600 AX 6300 range.

## Weights established

| Model | Weight | Exact reference | Printed / PDF page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FC-6207 | 672 g | 170 mm crank arms, 52/42T double, excluding the separately listed BB parts | 106 / 107 |
| FD-6300 | 107 g | Band-type front derailleur, 28.6 mm clamp | 42 / 43 |
| RD-6207-GS | 217 g | Long-cage assembly; distinct from the 195 g short cage | 25 / 26 |
| BR-6207-57 | 353 g | Complete front/rear 57-type pair, factory NBM pads and mounting bolts | 123 / 124 |
| BL-6207 | 213 g | Complete left/right lever pair, rubber covers included | 136 / 137 |
| HP-6207 | 150 g | Complete sealed threaded road headset, alloy cups/nut and chromed steel cone | 147 / 148 |

FC-6207 and the BB parts have separately published figures. The crankset's 672 g is not obtained by subtracting the BB reference. FD-6300's 28.6 mm band is established directly by its specification, while FD-6207's separate clamp options do not establish a single weighed band. BL-6207's weighed clamp size and HP-6207's weighed thread variant are not identified and remain explicit limitations.

## Caliper variants

The table explicitly assigns **353 g to front and rear, 57 type**. BR-6207-49 is added as a distinct complete pair with an unknown weight; the 57-type total is not transferred, divided or doubled. The period page and the [Shimano-hosted BR-6207 drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/BR-6207-49_6207-57-0604/EV-BR-6207-49_6207-57-0604C.pdf) identify complete factory shoe and pivot-bolt assemblies, supporting pad/bolt inclusion for the known weight.

The actual adjustment ranges are **39–49 mm** for the 49 type and **47–57 mm** for the 57 type. The catalogue says sunken pivot bolts are standard on the short-49 type, but does not identify the weighed bolt variant for the 57-type reference. That limitation is retained in the weight description; mounting style alone is not used to invent another weight.

## Derailleur specifications and the fork-end condition

| Record | Total capacity | Largest sprocket | Additional scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing RD-6207 short cage | 28T | 28T | Retains the previously sourced 195 g; smallest sprocket and separate front difference remain unknown |
| New RD-6207-GS long cage | 34T | 34T | The 34T largest-sprocket limit requires Shimano **SFR or PR fork ends**, per the source footnote |
| Existing RD-6300 | 28T | 28T | Source corroborates its existing 218 g; no smallest-sprocket or separate front-difference value is inferred |

RD-6207-GS uses the actual manufacturer suffix and its own [GS assembly drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/RD-6207-GS-0614/EV-RD-6207-GS-0614E.pdf). Its fork-end condition appears both in the weight description and specifications. Neither its weight nor its conditional sprocket limit replaces the short-cage reference.

The existing FD-6207 gains **3–18T** chainring capacity limits, and FD-6300 gains **14T** capacity. FD-6207's 110 g figure is withheld because the page lists 28 and 28.6 mm clamp options without identifying the weighed one. The similarly named FD-6300 has its own explicit 28.6 mm band and 107 g reference; models and masses remain separate.

## New records with unknown weights

- **BB-6207:** the crank/BB page gives 326 g for BB parts and lists 116 mm spindles for 68 mm shells and 119 mm spindles for 70 mm shells. It does not identify the weighed option, so neither is assigned the figure. Both available spindle lengths are recorded. The [FC-6207/BB-6207 drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/FC-6207_BB-6207-0603/EV-FC-6207_BB-6207-0603E.pdf) supplies the manufacturer-hosted assembly reference.
- **SL-6207-BA:** complete left/right band A friction downtube controls with 28.6 mm clamp. The table lists 63 g for this model without explicitly establishing the complete pair weight basis; the figure is not doubled. The [shared Shimano drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/SL-6207-0596/EV-SL-6207-0596.pdf) identifies front/rear controls and mount variants. The other mounts are not assigned this reference by compatibility alone.
- **PD-6207:** the page gives 220 g including a size L toe clip but does not establish a complete left/right pair basis. The new record identifies a pedal pair with that clip reference and keeps its weight null. The nearby PD-6300 figure and the earlier PD-7300 figure do not resolve the pair basis and are not used as substitutes.
- **BR-6207-49:** only its own short-reach pair identity and dimensions are established; its mass remains unknown as described above.

All new primary technical-document URLs are Shimano-hosted. The supplied scan is supporting evidence from an original manufacturer publication, not a new primary-document archive exception. No PDF bytes or source images are committed. This is a checked batch of selected 600-series pages, not a complete audit of all 162 pages.

## 1986 follow-up

The [1986 catalogue review](shimano-1986.md) resolves PD-6207’s mass with a directly published 440 g pair including size-L toe clips, adds the 6208 SIS revision and keeps the later 667 g Biopace double separate from the earlier 672 g round-ring crank reference.
