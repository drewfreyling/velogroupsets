# Shimano 2014: XTR and Deore weights, XT and SLX limits

Reviewed on **24 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 2014 Bicycle Components USA catalogue](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/2014_Bicycle_Components_usa.pdf). The PDF contains **284 image-scanned pages**. PDF and printed page numbers match on the pages cited below; figures were checked against the scanned tables and product pages.

This focused pass adds **eight components, all with qualified manufacturer weights**, and fills **18 previously unknown rear-derailleur limit fields across six existing records**. It adds no groupsets. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, **277 groupsets and 2,125 components**. Existing IDs, model numbers, positive weights and primary documents are preserved.

The [XTR product page, 20](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/2014_Bicycle_Components_usa.pdf#page=20), identifies these ten-speed models and their cage-specific masses and capacities. The [specification table, 196](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/2014_Bicycle_Components_usa.pdf#page=196), independently repeats those figures and supplies the sprocket/front-difference limits. Its “Low sprocket Max.” is the largest permitted sprocket; “Top sprocket Min.” is the smallest permitted sprocket. The other endpoints in those two rows are not confused with the required limits.

**RD-M981 is not RD-M980.** These are new model identities within the existing XTR M980 range. No figure is transferred to the original RD-M980 record, and SHADOW RD+ M986 remains distinct from SHADOW RD M981. XTR M980 inventory grows from four to nine components.

Page 20 also explicitly assigns **255 g to CS-M980, ten-speed 11–34T**. Its separately offered 11–36T option does not receive this weight. Lockring inclusion follows the contribution convention; an unprinted individual tooth sequence is not invented.

## Withheld figures and remaining review

This is a focused MTB follow-up, not a complete import of all 284 pages. Detailed tables elsewhere in this edition remain candidates for further review.

The XTR crank product-page figures do not establish every required weighed chainring/configuration detail; they are not used to fill crank weights. Later SL-M980 suffix revisions are not aliases for the original shifter pair. Hub figures without explicit skewer exclusion and disc-brake figures without the required complete assembly configuration remain withheld. No unprinted limit is inferred from cage length, gear combinations or chainring arithmetic.
