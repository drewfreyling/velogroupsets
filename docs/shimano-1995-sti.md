# Shimano 1995 STI Alivio, Acera-X and STX RC review

Reviewed on **23 November 2026** from the [manufacturer-hosted 1995 Shimano STI English/Spanish manual](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/STI/1993-2012_STI/1995_Shimano_STI_ENG-Spanish_Manual.pdf). The PDF contains **156 pages**; its archive disclaimer dates the information to 1995. References distinguish PDF pages from printed section/page numbers. Selected entries were cross-checked against the original [1995 dealer manual, pages 32–33](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1995_Shimano_Dealer_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=32).

This pass adds **11 components** and enriches **10 existing records**, with **no new weights or groupsets**. Alivio MC12 expands from four to eleven components; Acera-X expands from three to ten. The catalogue now contains four manufacturers, 272 groupsets and **1,995 components**. Existing identities, masses, primary technical documents and range start years are preserved.

The unsuffixed **ST-MC12** and **ST-MC15** identities are retained separately from the later **ST-MC12-C / ST-MC15-C** references. The manuals do not establish that the suffix can be discarded. Each new control record represents a complete left/right pair, rather than a single side with a doubled mass.

The Acera-X STI rear-drive sheet lists **CS-HG40**, while the dealer table lists **CS-HG50-C**. They are not treated as aliases. This pass adds the exact CS-HG40 cassette configuration from the STI sheet; it does not rename it to the dealer table's model or transfer a neighbouring cassette's mass.

## Filled specifications and shared components

Both the STI Acera-X rear-drive table and dealer page 33 explicitly give **RD-M290 GS** all four limits: **35T total capacity, 28T largest sprocket, 11T smallest sprocket and 18T front difference**, with seven-speed indexing. Dealer page 32 independently supplies the same four values for **RD-MC12**, without requiring an inferred cage code. These eight previously unknown fields are transcribed directly, not calculated from gearing.

**FD-M290** and **FD-MC12** gain triple/seven-speed references, 18T capacity, 42T maximum chainring, 8T top/middle minimum and their actual available clamp options. The smaller band is explicitly described as a **28.0–28.6 mm range**, separately from the 31.8 mm option and FD-MC12's additional 34.9 mm band. FD-MC12 and the existing **FD-MC33** gain the service table's 47.5/50 mm chainline options. Available options do not identify a weighed mount.

**FC-MC12** and **FC-M290** gain selected **170 mm, 42/34/24T references excluding the separately listed bottom bracket**, while preserving 175 mm as an available arm length. FC-MC12 also gains its explicitly printed 67 mm bolt circle. No weight is assigned from those dimensions.

The named Alivio column in dealer page 32 establishes **FH-MC12** and **CS-IG50 ag, seven-speed 11–28T**, so the existing identities gain Alivio MC12 membership. **BB-LP25** gains the separately named Alivio MC12 and Acera-X memberships from the front-drive tables. The records are shared rather than duplicated, and their descriptions are made neutral where necessary. No source-specific spindle option is silently made a weighed configuration.

## Weight limits and remaining review

The selected STI sheets and dealer specification tables supply no new qualifying component masses. Cranks remain unknown rather than borrowing another model's weight; hubs remain unknown pending figures explicitly excluding skewers. New cantilever records use complete front/rear pair references, including pads and mounting bolts when a compliant mass becomes available.

This is a focused inventory/specification review. XTR, XT/LX, remaining STX RC controls/rear-drive models, RSX, pedals and the Spanish duplicate sections remain further review material. Internal-gear and hybrid systems are not forced into road or MTB categories. The manual's line-up labels do not by themselves justify changing the existing range start years.
