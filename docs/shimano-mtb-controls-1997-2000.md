# Shimano MTB controls and derailleur specifications, 1997–2000

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 using original manufacturer-hosted dealer manuals. This batch adds eleven records and eleven manufacturer-listed weights: eight weights on new records and three previously unknown standalone shifter-pair weights. Three new cage-specific rear-derailleur records retain unknown weights. The catalogue now contains 1,411 components across the existing 257 groupsets and four manufacturers.

Existing IDs, slugs, model numbers, groupset memberships and primary technical documents are preserved. New records represent distinct manufacturer model numbers or explicit cage suffixes, rather than renaming a combined control to a standalone shifter or applying one cage's weight to all versions.

The 1997 table explicitly labels its combined-control weight row “Weight (pair)”; BL-M739 is separately marked “159 g (pair)”. The 2000 table identifies complete lever sets, with both the triple front and nine-speed rear controls specified in each set's column. Its standalone SL columns say “shift lever set only”; the brake levers have their own model and pair-weight rows. The figures are complete set references, not a doubled right-hand weight or an estimated deduction from an ST assembly. Cable/housing inclusion is not specified in the 2000 table and remains an explicit limitation.

The existing SL-M950 and SL-M739 weights remain null: their 1997 weight cells are blank. Their records now cite this limitation rather than inheriting the ST-M950 or ST-M739 total. ST-M910, SL-M952 and other nearby model codes are not treated as interchangeable with the reviewed models.

## Rear derailleurs

The [1997 table on page 30](https://na.s-tec.shimano.com/s3_assets/filelibrary/Catalogs/1990-2000_TSS_Manuals/1997_Shimano_Dealer_Sales_Support_Manual.pdf#page=30) gives distinct eight-speed cage columns:

| New record | Weight | Total capacity | Largest sprocket | Smallest sprocket | Maximum front difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RD-M950-GS | 207 g | 33T | 32T | 11T | 22T |
| RD-M950-SGS | Not yet sourced | 38T | 32T | 11T | 22T |
| RD-M739-GS | Not yet sourced | 33T | 32T | 11T | 22T |
| RD-M739-SGS | Not yet sourced | 39T | 32T | 11T | 20T |

The 207 g figure is printed only in RD-M950-GS's weight cell; the other three cells are blank. It is not transferred to SGS or the XT models. The existing generic RD-M950 and RD-M739 records retain unknown weights and capacity fields where cage selection matters. Both gain the common 32T largest / 11T smallest sprocket limits; RD-M950 also gains the 22T front difference common to its two columns. RD-M739's generic front difference remains null because GS and SGS differ.

## Front derailleurs

The same manufacturer table supplies missing chainring specifications:

| Existing model | Capacity | Minimum top-to-middle difference | Listed large-chainring options | Rear compatibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FD-M950 | 22T | 12T | 46T, 48T | Eight-speed |
| FD-M739 | 22T | 10T | 42T, 44T | Eight-speed |

Both weights remain unknown. Their table lists multiple clamp-band options without identifying the weighed diameter, so its mass figures do not satisfy the [front-derailleur weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md#front-derailleurs). E-type assemblies have separate columns and are not used to supply a band-mounted assembly's weight or configuration.

This is a checked selection of the tables, not a complete audit of either manual. The existing [dealer-manual index](shimano-dealer-manuals.md) and [earlier source reviews](catalogue-review-2026-11-21.md) contain further research leads.
