# Santé and Dura-Ace: the supplied 1988 brochure

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 from the [original Shimano brochure scan supplied by the project owner](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DjMjm22K9deQRXb2jHrivPKqs0Y_FAYr/view). The file contains 36 PDF pages. Its final page states copyright August 1988 and a `1088` printing code. References below use **PDF page numbers**, without inferring printed pagination or using the scan's creation timestamp as its publication year.

This batch adds eight Santé component records and fills four existing Santé weight gaps, for **eight newly sourced weights** in total. The complete catalogue now contains 1,435 records. Existing IDs, slugs, model numbers, group memberships and primary technical documents are preserved.

## Hub figures and the skewer rule

The brochure does contain the hub figures the project owner identified:

| Brochure reference | Published figure | What the publication establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FH-7402, eight-speed rear | 460 g | PDF p. 10 says rear without sprockets; it does not establish quick-release exclusion |
| FH-7400-6/7, 126 mm rear | 439 g | PDF p. 10 says rear without sprockets; it does not establish quick-release exclusion |
| HB-7400-F, front | 228 g | PDF p. 10 lists a front-hub figure and describes quick-release parts, without specifying skewer exclusion |
| FH-5000, seven-speed rear | 430 g | PDF p. 15 says rear without sprockets, not without skewers |
| HB-5000, front | 232 g | PDF p. 15 describes the QR lever and covered QR nut without specifying that they are excluded |

These figures are documented here as source findings, **not published as canonical hub weights**. The [hub guidelines](weights-guidelines.md#hubs) require weights without skewers. No quick-release weight is estimated, subtracted or borrowed from another model. The existing Dura-Ace hub records retain null weights with the source limitation explained. New HB-5000-F and FH-5000-7 records likewise retain unknown weights, while adding the documented 100/126 mm over-locknut dimensions, 28/32/36-hole options and seven-speed rear configuration.

For FH-7402, the existing [1991 dealer-manual citation, PDF p. 23 / printed p. 20](https://vintagecannondale.com/cannondale/shimano/1991%20Shimano.pdf#page=23) is more specific: it explicitly lists 460 g **with quick release**. That evidence and explanation are retained alongside the 1988 source; the new brochure does not supersede it.

The full Santé model suffixes come from the Shimano-hosted [HB-5000-F drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/HB-5000-F-0835/EV-HB-5000-F-0835A.pdf) and [FH-5000-7 drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/FH-5000-7_CS-5000-7-0846/EV-FH-5000-7_CS-5000-7-0846C.pdf); the brochure abbreviates them to HB-5000 and FH-5000. Their model numbers are not invented position suffixes. The combined assembly weight in [SI-R-3](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/si/R-3/SI-R-3-000-00-ENG.pdf) is not used to derive a hub-only weight either.

## Weights added

| Model | Weight | Exact weighed reference | PDF page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FC-5000 | 689 g | 170 mm arms, 52/42T Biopace double, excluding the separately listed BB set | 15 |
| BB-5000 | 295 g | Complete square-taper bottom-bracket set, 108 mm spindle | 15 |
| FD-5000 | 100 g | FD-5000-B band-type reference, 28.6 mm clamp | 15 |
| HP-5000 | 100 g | Complete threaded headset, 32.5 mm total / 10.5 mm lower stack | 15 |
| RD-5001-SS | 191 g | Short cage, six/seven-speed SIS | 14 |
| BR-5000-49 | 310 g | Complete front/rear pair with factory pads and sunken pivot bolts, 39–49 mm reach | 15 |
| BL-5001 | 266 g | Complete left/right aero brake-lever pair, 97.5 mm lever reach | 15 |
| CS-5000 | 233 g | Seven-speed 12–21T sprockets and spacers | 15 |

FC-5000 and BB-5000 have **separate published weights**. The crankset figure is not calculated by subtracting the bottom bracket from a combined assembly. The [FC-5000/BB-5000 drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/FC-5000_BB-5000-0836/EV-FC-5000_BB-5000-0836.pdf) confirms 108 mm spindle entries for both 68 and 70 mm shells, matching the brochure's `68-W108` / `70-W108` notation. The weighed BB thread/shell variant remains unspecified; the required spindle length is established.

The existing unsuffixed FD-5000 record retains its identity while explicitly identifying FD-5000-B as the weighed clamp configuration. The 100 g value is not presented as a braze-on FD-5000-F reference. HP-5000's weighed thread variant and BL-5001's weighed clamp size remain unspecified where the publication lists options. Cassette lockring inclusion is assumed under the weight guidelines; no sprocket sequence is invented from the other combinations listed on the page. SI-R-3 independently identifies CS-5000 as the seven-speed cassette system and remains its manufacturer-hosted technical document.

The [BR-5000 drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/BR-5000-0822/EV-BR-5000-0822D.pdf) explicitly names BR-5000-49 and shows complete front/rear shoe and bolt assemblies. The brochure identifies the 310 g pair as the **sunken-pivot-bolt** type. The optional standard bolts do not get that reference weight. The total is neither divided between positions nor doubled.

## Distinct models and remaining unknowns

- The new [RD-5001-SS/LS drawing reference](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/RD-5001-0840/EV-RD-5001-0840A.pdf) establishes the actual SS and LS suffixes. SS has 24T total capacity, a 24T largest sprocket and a 12T smallest sprocket. LS has 28T total capacity, a documented 23–28T largest-sprocket range and a 12T smallest sprocket. Neither separate maximum front-chainring difference is specified, so those fields remain null.
- The existing RD-5000 is a different model. The 191 g short-cage RD-5001-SS reference is not transferred to it. RD-5001-LS also retains an unknown weight.
- BL-5002 is added as a separate complete short-reach pair, with 87.5 mm lever reach. The 266 g figure is explicitly labelled for BL-5001 only, so BL-5002's weight stays null.
- New hub records are useful inventory additions even though the available weight figures do not satisfy the skewer rule. Their canonical weights stay null.

All new primary technical-document links are Shimano-hosted. The Drive scan is supporting evidence from the original manufacturer publication; no new archive exception or copied source images are introduced.
