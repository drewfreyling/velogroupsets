# Campagnolo groupsets from the 1980s and 1990s

This expansion adds period road groupsets alongside the existing [Record 8- and 9-speed records](campagnolo-record.md). Separate family records distinguish early groupsets from their later indexed offerings. Coverage is a sourced selection, not a claim that every Campagnolo group or component variant is listed.

## The 1980s road groups

| Family | Catalogue group number | Start year | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C-Record corsa | 180 | 1985 | Catalogue n.18 bis, April 1985, page 6 |
| Victory corsa | 269 | 1985 | Same catalogue, page 10 |
| Triomphe corsa | 267 | 1985 | Same catalogue, page 14 |
| Chorus | 700 | 1987 | July 1987 brochure, scan 2 |
| Croce d’Aune | B000 | 1987 | November 1987 brochure, scan 11 |
| Athena | D000A | 1989 | April 1989 brochure, scan 9 |

The dates identify availability in these dated manufacturer publications; they are not claims of an exact launch month or that earlier versions did not exist. In particular, 1985 is the documented reference year for the first three groups, and 1989 for this Athena offering. The model numbers are the actual complete-group catalogue numbers, rather than invented generation codes or a fixed sprocket count. Friction and period Syncro alternatives do not make these early families equivalent to their later eight-speed namesakes.

There are **41 new early component identities**: cranksets, bottom brackets, headsets, pedal pairs, seatposts, downtube shifter pairs, the C-Record/Victory/Triomphe rear derailleurs, Croce d’Aune LG rear derailleur, two Croce d’Aune/Athena clamp front derailleurs, and Athena Compact brake-lever pair. Victory and Triomphe share the documented 416/000 seatpost; Croce d’Aune and Athena share C0R2, C022 and C023. The latter two identify the fixed 28.5 mm clamp and adjustable 28–33 mm clamp respectively. Combined front/rear hub-set codes are not repurposed as individual hub model numbers, and combined brake-set codes are not used as standalone lever identifiers.

B010-LG has a published 33T total capacity and 30T largest sprocket in the November 1987 brochure, scan 5. Its smallest sprocket remains unknown. The table separately states an 18T *front-changer* capacity; this is not silently copied into an independent rear-derailleur front-difference limit. The other early rear-derailleur limits remain unknown. Some other derailleurs in these brochures lack an unambiguous individual component number in the reviewed parts lists and are not added by guessing one from a subgroup code.

These records use original manufacturer brochures preserved by Disraeli Gears: [April 1985 catalogue](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_-_catalogue_n_18_bis_april_1985_version.html), [July 1987 Chorus](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_-_chorus_july_1987_version.html), [November 1987 Croce d’Aune](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_-_croce_daune_november_1987_version.html), and [April 1989 Athena](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_-_athena.html). Source review on 8 November 2026 checked the original scanned group lists and component codes, rather than taking archive commentary as a technical specification. The April 1985 publication date is printed on page 55. The technical-document exception is limited to the exact page/scan paths for these four publications; see the [archive policy](campagnolo-record.md#approved-archived-manufacturer-documents).

The original manufacturer publications are archived by Disraeli Gears: [1992 road range](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_-_1992_road_range.html), [1993 parts](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_spare_parts_catalogue_-_1993_product_range.html), [1995 parts](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_spare_parts_catalogue_-_1995_product_range.html), and [1997 parts](https://www.disraeligears.co.uk/site/campagnolo_spare_parts_catalogue_-_1997.html). These establish documented availability; a family's start year does not date every later revision to that year. In particular, the 1991 eight-speed offerings predate some of the Ergopower controls listed here.

The component records describe the **1997 reference edition**, whose individual pages fall within the [approved archive exception](campagnolo-record.md#approved-archived-manufacturer-documents). There are 50 new component identities across these seven families. Chorus has distinct eight- and nine-speed rear derailleurs, Ergopower pairs and rear freehubs; its contemporary crankset, front derailleur, bottom brackets, headset, front hub and seatpost retain shared identities.

| Cranksets | 13–16, 18, 20 |
| Rear derailleurs | 23, 25–30 |
| Front derailleurs | 32–34, 36 |
| Ergopower lever pairs | 39, 41–45 |
| Avanti standalone brake-lever pair | 54 |
| Front hubs and rear freehubs | 58–60 |

Mirage and Avanti model identifiers retain the printed `I` in the 1997 drawings, such as `RDI11MI`, `ECI22AV` and `FHI20AV`; it is not silently replaced with a hyphen. Italian and English bottom brackets have separate model identities. Drawings showing shared replacement parts do not prove that complete assemblies belong to multiple groupsets. In particular, the Mirage front hub is not assigned to Avanti, and generic cassettes are not assigned by compatibility alone. Racing Triple remains a separate group; Veloce, Mirage and Avanti triple variants have not been added to their double records.

The initial expansion left all 91 weights unknown. The subsequent [1997 technical-specification review](campagnolo-1997-specifications.md) adds 24 compliant manufacturer-listed weights within these families, alongside six in the existing Record records, and sourced rear-derailleur capacities and largest sprockets. The 1980s records are unaffected. Other weights and unsupported limits remain explicitly unknown. Hub weights require documented skewer exclusion, square-taper bottom brackets identify spindle length, and crank weights require length and chainrings without including the bottom bracket. Every shifter record represents a left/right pair. No limit is inferred from another group, cage appearance or compatible parts. See the [weight guidelines](weights-guidelines.md).

## Earlier models sold during the 1980s

The [1982 Olympic catalogue review](campagnolo-1980s-weights.md) adds Super Record 0102018 (**182 g**), Nuovo Record 1020/A (**199 g**) and Nuovo Gran Sport 3500 (**220 g**). These are three additional identities outside the 41-component C-Record/Victory/Triomphe/Chorus/Croce d’Aune/Athena selection above. They use the existing older family records and do not supply weights for the later models.
