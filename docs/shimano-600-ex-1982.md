# Shimano 600 EX: April 1982 catalogue review

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 from the [original Shimano Bicycle System Components catalogue scan supplied by the project owner](https://drive.google.com/file/d/17QiI_JPy9NX1NiRCOWtYQDXxmGbQAJ5N/view). The scan has 144 PDF pages; the final page states “© Apr. 1982 by Shimano Industrial Co., Ltd” and the print code `0482 C1/22M`. The PDF creation timestamp is not the publication date. The 600 EX section occupies printed pages 50–57 / PDF pages 53–60.

This review adds ten records to the existing **600 EX 6200** group and fills the existing FD-6200 weight. The group now has thirteen records; the full catalogue has 1,427 components. Existing identities, group memberships and primary technical documents remain unchanged. No records are assigned to the later 6207 generation merely because their model numbers start with 62.

## Brake calipers and levers

The original specification and exploded assembly on **printed page 55 / PDF page 58** identify separate front and rear calipers:

| Model | Position / basis | Weight | Reach / configuration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BR-6200 | Front | 159 g | CS-57 type, 43–57 mm |
| BR-6200 | Rear | 157 g | CS-57 type, 43–57 mm |
| BR-6210 | Front | 150 g | CS-49 type, 39–49 mm |
| BR-6210 | Rear | 147 g | CS-49 type, 39–49 mm |
| BL-6200 | Complete left/right pair | 209 g | Rubber covers explicitly included; 23.8 mm handlebar clamps |

The caliper figures describe complete factory assemblies with NBM brake shoes and pivot/mounting bolts. Both the period catalogue and manufacturer drawings show the complete shoe and bolt assemblies. Optional sunken pivot bolts are listed separately; the weighed mounting-bolt/nut variant is not specified, and that limitation is retained. The four position records preserve the actual manufacturer model numbers BR-6200 and BR-6210. Position appears in the stable ID and description, not an invented manufacturer suffix. Front/rear weights are not divided, doubled or combined into an additional pair record.

Primary technical documents use Shimano-hosted originals:

- [BR-6200 exploded view](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/BR-6200-SHIMANO%20600%20EX-0319/EV-BR-6200-SHIMANO%20600%20EX-0319G.pdf): 57-type reach, factory shoes and separate front/rear pivot assemblies.
- [BR-6210 exploded view](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/BR-6210-0589/EV-BR-6210-0589B.pdf): 49-type reach, factory shoes and front/rear mounting hardware.
- [BL-6200 exploded view](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/BL-6200-0367/EV-BL-6200-0367B.pdf): hooded lever assembly and rubber covers.

The later service-drawing revision dates are not used as introduction dates or as the source of the 1982 weights.

## Other missing components and specifications

| Model | Record / result | Original page, printed / PDF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HP-6200 | New complete threaded headset; manufacturer-listed 150 g, labyrinth seals; weighed thread variant unspecified | 57 / 60 |
| SL-6200 | New complete left/right friction downtube shifter pair, band mount; weight unknown | 52 / 55 |
| SL-6210 | New complete left/right friction downtube shifter pair, braze-on A mount; weight unknown | 52 / 55 |
| BB-6200 | New square-taper bottom bracket, 116 mm spindle; weight unknown | 54 / 57 |
| BB-6210 | New square-taper bottom bracket, 119 mm spindle; weight unknown | 54 / 57 |
| FD-6200 | Existing record gains 112 g for the identified 1⅛-inch clamp, plus 14T capacity | 51 / 54 |
| RD-6200 | Existing short-cage record gains 28T total capacity, 28T maximum sprocket and 13T maximum front difference; retains its 190 g weight | 50 / 53 |

FD-6200's clamp is specified in inches in both the table and the clamp assembly listing. The record retains 1.125 inches and its exact conversion, 28.575 mm; this is a unit conversion, not an additional measurement. Its existing Shimano-hosted primary drawing is preserved. RD-6200's smallest-sprocket field remains null because this page does not state it; the short-cage weight is not transferred to RD-6210.

The headset uses the [HP-7200/HP-6200 Shimano drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/HP-7200_6200-0466/EV-HP-7200_6200-0466.pdf) for exact HP-6200 assembly identification; its 600 EX membership and 150 g reference come from the 1982 catalogue. The drawing's 600 AX heading does not override the catalogue's explicit 600 EX listing or establish additional group membership by itself.

The [shared SL-6200/6210/6220 drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/SL-6200_6210_6220-0322/EV-SL-6200_6210_6220-0322E.pdf) identifies the complete front/rear lever assemblies and distinct mounts. The catalogue gives 58 g for the band-type reference without explicitly identifying its pair weight basis. That figure is withheld rather than doubled or applied to SL-6210. The unlisted SL-6220 mount is not assigned membership solely from the shared service drawing.

BB-6200 and BB-6210 are explicitly named with their spindle lengths alongside FC-6200. Their manufacturer-hosted [FC-6200 assembly drawing](https://si.shimano.com/en/pdfs/ev/FC-6200-0329/EV-FC-6200-0329G.pdf) identifies the associated cups, bearings and spindle assemblies; no separate BB weight is given. FC-6200 remains unweighed because the source does not establish a crankset-only figure with exact arm length and chainring configuration. Other available spindle/shell combinations in the drawing do not become additional weight references.

The Google Drive scan is supporting evidence from an original manufacturer publication. It does not become an approved non-manufacturer primary technical-document host, and no source images or PDF bytes are copied into this repository.
