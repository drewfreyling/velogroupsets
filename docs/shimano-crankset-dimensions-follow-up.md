## Configuration details

- **CUES FC-U6030-2 and FC-U6040-2:** the joint drawings specify 110 mm for the 40/42/46/50T rings and 80 mm for 32/34T inner rings. The catalogued double-ring configurations receive both diameters; no single-ring model is created or assigned an inner ring.
- **Ultegra triples:** FC-6603 and FC-6604-G use 130 / 74 mm; FC-6703 uses 130 / 92 mm. These are separate, explicitly printed model columns.
- **Saint FC-M800 variants:** the 2005/2008 tables explicitly list 104 / 64 mm for -1, -2 and -3. For the -1, this records the manufacturer’s published crank mounting circles, including the unused small circle; it does not claim a second chainring is fitted. The existing single/double/triple identities and reference chainrings remain unchanged.
- **FC-M600, FC-M800 and FC-M810:** retain their existing catalogue identities and linked service assemblies. Their drawing dimensions do not rename them or transfer a weight between variants.
- **FC-A050:** the 2008 manufacturer table explicitly lists 130 mm. A published mounting-circle dimension alone does not establish that the rings are separately replaceable; no such serviceability claim is added.
- **FC-M572:** the exact unsuffixed model column on 2005 handbook PDF page 55 gives 104 / 64 mm. Its weight is still unknown; a similarly named -S column on the preceding page is not treated as a new weight reference.

## Scope and remaining gaps

BCD describes a dimension, not complete chainring compatibility. Bolt layout, manufacturer ring design and other mounting requirements still apply. The 31 records now have dimensional citations; their existing unknown weights stay unknown wherever an exact crank length, chainring combination or bottom-bracket exclusion remains unproven.

Across the two consecutive BCD passes, **94 existing cranksets** gained sourced dimensions. The catalogue now has numeric BCDs for **157 cranksets**, with **20 additional documented mounting descriptions**. These counts describe the current catalogue, not complete manufacturer coverage. Other unknown diameters remain omitted and display “Not yet sourced”.
